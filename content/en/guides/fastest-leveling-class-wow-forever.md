In WoW Forever, leveling is a journey through classic Azeroth, and unlike in current WoW your class makes a noticeable difference. It mostly comes down to downtime: if you have to eat and drink after every fight, you progress more slowly than someone whose pet does the work.

> **Our basis:** WoW Forever launches on November 4, 2026 and builds on WoW Classic. This guide is therefore based on Classic experience. We assume it largely applies to Forever, but that is an assumption. After launch we will check whether new talents or changes affect it.

Playing current WoW? There is a separate guide: [fastest leveling class in WoW Midnight](/en/guides/fastest-leveling-class-wow).

## What makes a class fast in Forever

- **Little downtime:** Eating, drinking and bandaging cost time. Classes with a pet or self-healing save it.
- **A pet that tanks:** When a beast or demon takes the hits, you barely lose health.
- **Area damage:** Defeating several enemies at once is faster, but it takes practice and good positioning.
- **Movement:** Until your first mount you walk a lot. Travel forms and speed abilities help noticeably.

## The fastest classes

::class-art-row{classes="hunter,warlock,mage,druid" mode="forever"}
::

### Hunter

Many consider the [Hunter](/en/forever/classes/hunter) the fastest leveling class in Classic, and that is unlikely to change much in Forever. Your pet holds enemies, you shoot from a safe distance and rarely need a break. [**Aspect of the Cheetah**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5118){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} speeds up travel between quests, and [**Feign Death**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5384){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} saves you when things get out of hand. In Forever, **Humans** can be Hunters for the first time.

### Warlock

The [Warlock](/en/forever/classes/warlock) is the second classic. The Voidwalker soaks damage, curses work over time, and [**Life Tap**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1454){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} turns health into mana, which you get back with [**Drain Life**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Hardly a second passes between fights. New in Forever: the **Troll Warlock**.

### Mage

The [Mage](/en/forever/classes/mage) can defeat many enemies at once, for example with [**Blizzard**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} or [**Arcane Explosion**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1449){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. That is very fast, but requires good positioning and an eye on your mana. In return, [**Conjure Water**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5504){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} provides your own drinks. New in Forever: the **Orc Mage**.

### Druid

The [Druid](/en/forever/classes/druid) is versatile: it heals itself, sneaks past enemies as a cat and travels faster in [**Travel Form**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=783){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Whether it is one of the fastest leveling classes is disputed in the community. Most recommend Feral Combat as the leveling tree.

## The middle of the pack

- **[Shaman](/en/forever/classes/shaman):** Heals itself, wears mail later and travels as a [**Ghost Wolf**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2645){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Solid and sturdy.
- **[Rogue](/en/forever/classes/rogue):** Quick damage and [**Stealth**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1784){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} to avoid enemies, but no self-healing. Food and bandages are part of the deal.
- **[Priest](/en/forever/classes/priest):** Decent as Shadow with [**Mind Flay**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15407){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and a wand, but needs breaks for mana.
- **[Paladin](/en/forever/classes/paladin):** Almost never dies, but defeats enemies rather slowly on its own. Safe rather than fast.

## The slowest solo: Warrior

The [Warrior](/en/forever/classes/warrior) is most likely the slowest class to level solo. It cannot heal itself, often needs food after fights and depends heavily on gear. In a group with a healer, that flips completely: then it is one of the strongest classes of all.

## Tips for less downtime

1. **Learn First Aid.** Bandages save a lot of time for every class without healing.
2. **Carry enough food and drink.** Better one stack too many than a trip back to town.
3. **Bundle quests.** Pick up every quest in an area and do them in one go.
4. **Find a group** for elite quests and dungeons. It gives lots of experience and is more fun.
5. **Use rested experience.** Log out in an inn or a city to get bonus experience next time.
