## Which healer suits you?

**For beginners:** **Holy Paladin** and **Holy Priest** have clear, direct heals and strong emergency abilities. The Paladin also benefits from plate armor.

**For planners:** the **Restoration Druid** heals ahead of time with effects over time. If you like thinking ahead, this is for you.

**For players who don't just want to heal:** **Discipline Priest** and **Mistweaver** combine damage and healing. You attack enemies and heal your group in the process.

**For the experimental:** the **Preservation Evoker** plays unlike any other healer thanks to its charge-up spells.

## Tips for new healers

1. **Positioning is everything.** Stand where you can reach everyone and stay out of danger zones.
2. **Not every health bar needs to be full.** Efficient healing saves Mana for the dangerous moments.
3. **Help with damage.** In quiet phases, every healer contributes damage too.
