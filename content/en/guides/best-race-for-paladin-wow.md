The Paladin is the class with the most order stories. Every race that has Paladins found its own path to the Light: the Humans through the Silver Hand, the Blood Elves through a captive naaru, the Tauren through the sun itself. In WoW Midnight **9 races** can play Paladin, and patch 12.2 adds three more. This guide covers the honest numbers for all three roles and the stories behind every order.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="dwarf,human,tauren,bloodelf,draenei,darkiron" reasons="Alliance: best race for Retribution, first pick for Holy and Protection|Alliance: PvP favorite, the Silver Hand|Horde: best race for Retribution and first Horde PvP pick|Horde: best Holy pick, the Blood Knights|Alliance: the vindicators, Gift of the Naaru|Alliance: Fireblood for tanks"}
::

- **[Retribution](/en/classes/paladin/retribution):** **Dwarf** (Alliance) and **Tauren** (Horde).
- **[Holy](/en/classes/paladin/holy):** **Dwarf**, for the Horde **Blood Elf**.
- **[Protection](/en/classes/paladin/protection):** **Dwarf** or **Dark Iron Dwarf**, otherwise purely a matter of taste.
- **PvP:** Alliance **Human**, Horde **Tauren**.
- **Lore:** **Human**, **Blood Elf**, **Tauren**, **Draenei**.

## How much does your race really matter?

Not much. For **Retribution**, the best race in the [Bloodmallet](https://bloodmallet.com/chart/paladin/retribution/races/castingpatchwerk) simulations (28 October 2026) is only **1.9 percent** ahead of the weakest. There are no damage sims for Holy and Protection, where Stoneform and Fireblood matter most. Method even describes race choice for Protection outside the top level as purely cosmetic.

| Role | Best Alliance pick | Best Horde pick | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Retribution](/en/classes/paladin/retribution) | Dwarf, Human | Tauren, Blood Elf | 1.9% gap best to weakest race |
| [Holy](/en/classes/paladin/holy) | Dwarf | Blood Elf | 45 of the 50 best M+ healers are Dwarves |
| [Protection](/en/classes/paladin/protection) | Dwarf, Dark Iron Dwarf | Blood Elf | Stoneform and Fireblood |

Lightforged and Earthen sit at the bottom of the Retribution list.

## The Alliance races

### Human: the Silver Hand

Archbishop Alonsus Faol founded the order of the **Silver Hand** in the Second War, with Uther the Lightbringer as its first leader. The first five Paladins were Uther, Turalyon, Tirion Fordring, Saidan Dathrohan and Gavinrad. Tirion was later cast out of the order for defending the orc Eitrigg, and Arthas Menethil, the most famous fallen Paladin of all, came from these ranks too. As a Human Paladin you stand in the oldest tradition of the class.

In PvP the Human is the clear Alliance pick: [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59752) frees you from any stun, and in the 3v3 ladder almost all of the 50 best Retribution players are Human. [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20598) gives 2 percent to all secondary stats and makes the Human the second-best Alliance pick for Retribution.

### Dwarf: ahead in every role

The Dwarves of Ironforge joined the Silver Hand after the Third War. A Dwarf Paladin with hammer and shield is the picture of a fighter who will not give ground.

In the numbers the Dwarf is number one: [Might of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59224) makes critical strikes **and critical heals** count for 204 instead of 200 percent, which makes the Dwarf the best race for Retribution. [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594) removes poison, disease, curse, magic and bleed effects and reduces physical damage taken. For **Holy** that is so valuable that 45 of the 50 best Holy Paladins in Mythic+ play a Dwarf, and as a **Protection** tank the Dwarf is the first pick too.

### Draenei: the vindicators

The very first Paladins may have been Draenei, who learned the Light from the naaru long before the Humans knew of it. Their vindicators of the Hand of Argus have fought the Burning Legion for thousands of years. Maraad is one of the best known; he fell in Warlords of Draenor defending Shattrath. [Gift of the Naaru](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59542) heals you for 20 percent of your health over 5 seconds, and [Heroic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6562) adds primary stat. Middle of the pack for Retribution.

### Lightforged Draenei: the Light in the body

The Lightforged took the Light itself into their bodies in a ritual and fought the war against the Legion with the **Army of the Light** across the Twisting Nether. **Turalyon**, one of the first five Paladins of the Silver Hand, is now a Lightforged human himself and High Exarch of the Army. In looks, the Lightforged Paladin is hard to beat.

The numbers tell a different story: [Light's Judgment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255647) is weak, and the Lightforged sit at the very bottom for Retribution. For healers, [Holy Providence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255651) with 1 percent more healing is a small bonus.

### Dark Iron Dwarf: the tank with Fireblood

[Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221) removes harmful effects and briefly raises your Strength, and [Forged in Flames](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265224) reduces physical damage taken. Method therefore recommends the Dark Iron Dwarf for **Protection** on the same level as the Dwarf, and it is the third Alliance pick for Retribution. And a Paladin with glowing veins of lava simply looks spectacular.

## The Horde races

### Blood Elf: the Blood Knights

After the fall of the Sunwell, the Blood Elves forced the captive naaru **M'uru** to give them the Light and founded the **Blood Knights**. Lady Liadrin was the first of them. After M'uru's sacrifice at the Sunwell, the Blood Knights found their way to a more honest faith and now serve the renewed Sunwell. In Midnight, Lady Liadrin falls fighting Kith'ix. Walking through Quel'Thalas as a Blood Knight today, you carry her legacy forward.

The Paladin gets its own version of [Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=155145): it dispels a buff from enemies and gives you **one Holy Power**. Together with [Arcane Acuity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154742) and its 1 percent critical strike, Method calls the Blood Elf the best Horde pick for **Holy**. In Mythic+ it is the second most common race for Protection.

### Tauren: the Sunwalkers

Tauren Paladins call themselves **Sunwalkers** and revere An'she, the sun, as one of the two eyes of the Earth Mother. The order was founded by Aponi Brightmane, the first tauren Paladin and a former warrior, together with Tahu Sagewind. In Midnight, Aponi helps channel the Light into the Sunwell. No other race has interpreted the Light so independently.

In the numbers the Tauren is the first Horde pick for **Retribution**: [Brawn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154743) makes critical strikes count for 204 instead of 200 percent, and on multiple targets the Tauren is even the best race overall. In PvP, [War Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20549) adds an area stun, and Skill-Capped recommends the Tauren for Retribution and Holy.

### Zandalari Troll: the prelates

The Zandalari prelates received their power from **Rezan, Loa of Kings**, and kept it through faith after his death. High Prelate Rata leads them. For [Embrace of the Loa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292752), pick [Pa'ku](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292361) for Retribution (Method's third Horde pick), [Akunda](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292359) with extra heals for **Holy**, and [Krag'wa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292364) with extra health and armor for **Protection**.

## Earthen: the Ordinants

The Earthen call their Paladins **Ordinants**. [Azerite Surge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436344) is an area attack with healing, and [Ingest Minerals](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436341) replaces food with gems. For Retribution the Earthen rank far behind in the simulations, but as an Alliance or Horde Paladin they are the only option with a titan look.

## Coming soon: Night Elves, Undead and Trolls (patch 12.2)

At BlizzCon 2026, Blizzard announced that with patch 12.2 Eclipse, **Night Elves, Undead and Darkspear Trolls** will be able to become Paladins. The patch is expected in early 2027. Each of these races gets dedicated quests that follow one member of that race on their path to the Light, and Blizzard also wants to explain how of all races the Undead find their way to the Light. As soon as these Paladins arrive, we will update this guide.

## Our tip

Pick the order that speaks to you: the Silver Hand of the Humans, the Blood Knights of Silvermoon, the Sunwalkers of the Tauren, the vindicators of the Draenei. Each tells a different story about the Light. If you play Holy in high Mythic+, there is hardly a way around the Dwarf, and in PvP around the Human. And if you would rather be a Night Elf, Undead or Troll: patch 12.2 is coming.
