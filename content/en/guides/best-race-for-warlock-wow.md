Hardly any class is as closely tied to one race as the Warlock is to the Orcs. Still, in WoW Midnight **all 26 races can play Warlock**, and none of them will make you noticeably weaker. This guide covers both: the honest numbers for everyone who wants the maximum, and the question of which race feels right as a Warlock, with lore, racial abilities and a few races for which summoning demons is really breaking a taboo.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="orc,human,troll,voidelf,gnome,undead" reasons="Horde: best race for Demonology, the original warlock race|Alliance: strong in Affliction and Demonology|Horde: best race for Affliction|Alliance: strong in all three specs, PvP favorite for Destruction|Alliance: Gnomeregan trained the warlocks of Khaz Modan|Horde: the dark lore pick, strong against fear in PvP"}
::

- **Numbers only:** [Demonology](/en/classes/warlock/demonology) **Orc** (Horde) or **Human** (Alliance), [Affliction](/en/classes/warlock/affliction) **Troll** or **Void Elf/Human**, [Destruction](/en/classes/warlock/destruction) **Dwarf** or **Tauren**. The neutral **Pandaren** is near the top everywhere.
- **Mythic+:** among top Affliction players, Orc, Night Elf, Blood Elf, Human and Void Elf are the most common, with no clear favorite.
- **PvP:** Alliance **Night Elf** or **Void Elf**, Horde **Highmountain Tauren** or **Orc**.
- **Lore and feel:** **Orc**, **Gnome**, **Undead**, **Blood Elf**, **Human**.

## How much does your race really matter?

Not much. In the [Bloodmallet](https://bloodmallet.com/chart/warlock/demonology/races/castingpatchwerk) simulations (28 October 2026, Season 2) the best race is only **1.8 to 2.1 percent** ahead of the weakest. Most races are less than **1 percent** apart. Method openly states for Demonology that the difference from the best to the worst race is 2 percent.

| Spec | Best race (Alliance) | Best race (Horde) | Gap best to weakest race |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Affliction](/en/classes/warlock/affliction) | Void Elf, Human | Troll | 1.8% |
| [Demonology](/en/classes/warlock/demonology) | Human | Orc | 1.9% |
| [Destruction](/en/classes/warlock/destruction) | Dwarf | Tauren | 2.1% |

Undead, Vulpera, Lightforged and Earthen are at the bottom here too, all only about 2 percent behind the top. A race that thrills you every time you look at your character is worth more than those 2 percent.

## The Horde races

### Orc: where it all began

The history of the Warlock is the history of the Orcs. On Draenor the demon lord Kil'jaeden corrupted **Gul'dan**, who became the first orc warlock and founded the **Shadow Council**. The orc warlocks drank the blood of Mannoroth, opened the Dark Portal and brought the Burning Legion all the way to Azeroth. A note for lore fans: Gul'dan's master Ner'zhul was a shaman, not a warlock, and was betrayed by Gul'dan.

In gameplay the Orc leads too: [Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=21563) increases your pets' damage, and a Demonology Warlock fights with a whole army of imps, felhounds and felguards. Add [Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33702) for more Intellect over 15 seconds and [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20573) against stuns. The result: best race for Demonology, strong in Affliction, and the second PvP pick for the Horde.

### Undead: fel magic to feel alive

Many Forsaken turn to fel magic because it gives them a sense of control, some even say a sense of being alive again. An undead warlock in Undercity is one of the most fitting images in all of WoW. In the numbers, though, Undead are at the very bottom. Their strength is PvP: [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7744) breaks fear, charm and sleep, which is worth gold against other Warlocks. [Touch of the Grave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5227) also drains life from enemies.

### Blood Elf: addicted to fel

After the fall of the Sunwell, the Blood Elves suffered from their addiction to magic, and many fed it with fel energy. Prince Kael'thas and his Sunfury walked that path to the bitter end. Because Midnight is set in Quel'Thalas, a Blood Elf Warlock is a character right in the middle of the expansion's story. [Arcane Acuity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154742) gives 1 percent critical strike, and [Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=28730) dispels buffs from enemies. Middle of the pack in the simulations.

### Troll: best Horde Affliction Warlock

Troll warlocks are **demoniacs** who bind demons inside their own bodies, a unique and eerie form of summoning. [Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=26297) gives 10 percent haste for 12 seconds and makes the Troll the best race for Affliction, while [Da Voodoo Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58943) shortens snares.

### Tauren and Highmountain Tauren

Tauren Warlocks are rare, and in the lore they stand for tribes like the Grimtotem who turned away from the Earth Mother. If you want to play one, [Brawn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154743) (critical strikes for 204 percent) makes the Tauren the best Horde race for **Destruction**. **Highmountain Tauren**, on the other hand, are the first PvP pick for the Horde: [Rugged Tenacity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255659) reduces every hit you take, and [Bull Rush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255654) knocks enemies down.

### Goblin

For Goblins, fel energy is above all one thing: cheap and renewable. A Goblin Warlock trading soulstones is pure Goblin logic. [Time is Money](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69042) gives 1 percent haste, [Rocket Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69041) some extra damage. Middle of the pack.

### Nightborne, Mag'har, Zandalari and Vulpera

- **Nightborne:** Suramar had the **Felborne**, who joined the Legion, with Star Augur Etraeus among the most powerful. First Arcanist Thalyssra has banned warlocks in Suramar ever since, so a Nightborne Warlock is an outsider. [Magical Affinity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255665) increases your magic damage by 1 percent, but in Demonology the Nightborne still rank far behind.
- **Mag'har Orc:** [Ancestral Call](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274738) briefly boosts one of your best secondary stats, and [Sympathetic Vigor](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=273217) gives your demon more health. Lower middle.
- **Zandalari Troll:** for [Embrace of the Loa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292492), definitely pick [Pa'ku](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292463), the others are far behind.
- **Vulpera:** no lore, no strong numbers, but a fox with a fel imp is simply wonderful.

## The Alliance races

### Human: the forbidden coven

In Stormwind, demonology is officially forbidden, and the Kirin Tor banned it centuries ago. Still, a secret coven of warlocks meets beneath **The Slaughtered Lamb**. Kanrethad Ebonlocke, one of the great warlocks of the Black Harvest, was Human. In gameplay the Human is the strongest Alliance pick for Demonology and ties with the Void Elf in Affliction: [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20598) gives 2 percent to all secondary stats, and [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59752) breaks stuns.

### Gnome: Gnomeregan's dark school

Gnomeregan once trained the warlocks of all of Khaz Modan, and Wilfred Fizzlebang is probably the best known (and unluckiest) Gnome Warlock, who accidentally summoned the eredar lord Lord Jaraxxus at the Trial of the Crusader. [Nimble Fingers](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=92680) gives 1 percent haste, [Expansive Mind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20591) 5 percent more mana, and [Escape Artist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20589) frees you from roots. Middle of the pack, but full of character.

### Void Elf: between Void and fel

Void Elves have learned to live with the Void without falling to it. As Warlocks they mix two dangerous powers, and it fits surprisingly well. [Entropic Embrace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255669) regularly boosts your damage by 5 percent, [Preternatural Calm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255670) stops spell pushback, and [Spatial Rift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=256948) teleports you. Tied with the Human at the top of Affliction, and more than every second Destruction Warlock in the 3v3 ladder is a Void Elf.

### Dwarf and Dark Iron Dwarf

A Dwarf Warlock counts as an oddity in the lore, while the Dark Iron Dwarves with their long history of dark magic fit much better. In the numbers it is the other way round: [Might of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59224) makes the Dwarf the best race for **Destruction**, plus [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594) for Mythic+. The **Dark Iron Dwarf** brings [Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221), a cleanse with a short stat boost.

### Night Elf

For Night Elves, fel magic is what almost destroyed their world in the War of the Ancients. A Night Elf Warlock is a real taboo breaker. In PvP it is still the first pick: [Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984) lets you dodge projectiles and crowd control.

### Draenei and Lightforged: the biggest taboo breakers

The Draenei only exist because they chose to stand **against** the demons thousands of years ago. That makes a Draenei Warlock perhaps the most exciting character on this list: a penitent eredar turning the weapon of their enemies against them. Since Dragonflight, the penitent eredar provide the lore foundation for it. [Heroic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6562) gives Intellect, and [Gift of the Naaru](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59548) heals you. Among the **Lightforged**, Ph'el Oman is the very first warlock, and in the numbers they rank low because of the weak [Light's Judgment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255647).

### Worgen, Kul Tiran and Mechagnome

- **Worgen:** [Viciousness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68975) gives 1 percent critical strike, [Darkflight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68992) makes you faster. A Worgen keeping their own beast in check with fel magic is a strong character.
- **Kul Tiran:** [Brush It Off](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=291628) gives 1 percent versatility. Small bonuses, lots of seafaring charm.
- **Mechagnome:** [Combat Analysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=312923) builds up more and more primary stat in combat, just behind Orc and Human in Demonology.

## The neutral races

- **Pandaren:** according to the lore, some Pandaren turn to fel magic seemingly just for fun. [Epicurean](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107072) doubles the stats from food and keeps the Pandaren among the top five in **every** spec.
- **Dracthyr:** the Dracthyr occultists study forbidden magic, and since The War Within they can be Warlocks too. [Awakened](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=365575) gives mastery, and [Soar](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=369536) makes you unbeatably fast in the open world.
- **Earthen:** some Earthen are drawn to the Cleft of Shadow. [Ingest Minerals](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436341) replaces food with gems. At the very bottom in the numbers.
- **Haranir:** the new race of Midnight, without warlock lore of its own yet. [Lash Out](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238623) increases your critical damage by 1 percent. Bottom half of the simulations.

## Our tip

The Warlock is the class with the best stories. An Orc following in Gul'dan's footsteps, a Draenei fighting with her enemies' weapons, a Gnome hoping that this time no eredar lord steps out of the portal. Pick the story you want to tell. You will make up the 2 percent with gear quickly.
