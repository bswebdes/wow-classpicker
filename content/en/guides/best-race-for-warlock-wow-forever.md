The Warlock in WoW Forever is not just Classic all over again. Your demons now scale with your own stats, Life Tap depends on your Spirit, and damage over time effects can crit. That shifts the question of the best race too. On top of that comes a new combination: the **Troll Warlock**.

This guide is based on Blizzard's development notes from 26 and 27 October 2026, the state shortly before launch on **4 November 2026**. We will check it against the live game after launch.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="orc,gnome,human,undead,troll" mode="forever" reasons="Horde: best PvE pick, Command strengthens your demons|Alliance: Eureka! for burst, strong in Destruction|Alliance: Spirit for Life Tap, Will to Survive in PvP|Horde: best PvP pick against fear|Horde: new in Forever, Berserking for burst"}
::

- **Horde PvE:** **Orc**, since Blizzard gave it back Command on 26 October.
- **Horde PvP:** **Undead** with Will of the Forsaken.
- **Alliance:** **Gnome** and **Human** are level, depending on how you play.
- **New:** the **Troll** is the fresh pick for anyone who wants something different.
- **Important:** none of these recommendations is based on simulations. The differences are about **1 percent**.

## What changes for Warlocks in Forever

Three changes from Blizzard's class deep dive directly affect your race choice:

- **Demons scale with your stats.** Better gear makes your imp or voidwalker stronger too. Everything that helps your pets becomes more valuable, above all the Orc's Command.
- **Life Tap scales with your Spirit.** That benefits the Human with its extra Spirit.
- **Pandemic lets damage over time effects crit.** Critical strike, for example from the Human's Ruthless, now also helps Affliction.

## The Horde races

### Orc: the original warlocks

The Orcs brought warlocks to Azeroth. In the time of WoW Forever they have freed themselves from the demonic blood curse under Thrall, but the warlocks have not vanished: they gather in the **Cleft of Shadow** beneath Orgrimmar, and in Durotar the Burning Blade cult is up to no good. An Orc Warlock carries on the darkest legacy of their people.

In gameplay, Blizzard gave the Orc **Command** back on 26 October: your pets deal 5 percent more damage. Because demons now grow with your stats, that is stronger than in Classic and makes the Orc the first PvE pick for the Horde (Wowhead has no page for the Forever version yet). Add [Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20572) with 10 percent more spell power for 15 seconds, without the old healing penalty. New is [Shatter Curse](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1299026), which removes curses, including the Warlock's new Banes, a real advantage against other Warlocks in PvP. [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20573) shortens stuns by 20 percent.

### Undead: the terror of PvP

The Forsaken have only just freed themselves from the Lich King, and many of them turn to dark magic to stand against him. An undead warlock in the Magic Quarter of Undercity is one of the most classic images in World of Warcraft.

[Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=7744) breaks fear, charm and sleep every 2 minutes. Against other Warlocks, whose fear is one of their strongest weapons, that makes you the first PvP pick for the Horde. [Cannibalize](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20577) restores mana as well in Forever, and [Touch of the Grave](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260189) has a 30 percent chance to drain life from your target when you cast.

### Troll: the new combination

There were never Troll Warlocks in the classic world. In WoW Forever they learn from the **orc warlock Nartok** in the Den in the Valley of Trials. In your [first class quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98575) he says: "We orcs have a long and turbulent history with the entities of the Nether," and that the Orcs now forge their own path. He also warns you: "The fearful lash out against that which they do not understand." As a Troll Warlock you will stand out among your people.

[Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20554) increases your casting speed by 10 percent for 15 seconds, and [Rapid Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260270) heals you for half your health over 6 seconds while you stand still. Add [Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20555) and [Beast Slaying](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20557) for 5 percent more damage against beasts. Mein-MMO sees the Troll as a good pick for Destruction.

## The Alliance races

### Gnome: Eureka! for Destruction

In the lore, Gnomeregan trained the warlocks of all of Khaz Modan, and even after the fall of their city the Gnomes stayed true to this dark art. In WoW Forever they get a new ability: [Eureka!](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259821) lowers your spell costs by 10 percent and increases your damage by 10 percent for 8 seconds.

The catch: since 1 October, Eureka! no longer affects periodic damage. According to Blizzard, channeled spells like Drain Life and Drain Soul still count. That makes the Gnome very strong as a **Destruction** Warlock with Shadow Bolt and Immolate, while the bonus is smaller for **Affliction** with its many damage over time effects. [Expansive Mind](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20591) gives 5 percent more mana, and [Escape Artist](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20589) frees you from roots and makes you immune to them for 3 seconds.

### Human: Spirit for Life Tap

Demonology is forbidden in Stormwind, yet a secret coven of warlocks meets beneath **The Slaughtered Lamb**. A Human who becomes a Warlock begins their career in the underground of the Alliance's greatest city.

Thanks to the changes in Forever, the Human is stronger than in Classic: [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20598) increases your Spirit by 10 percent, and your Life Tap now depends on Spirit. [Ruthless](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20597) gives 1 percent critical strike, which thanks to Pandemic also benefits your damage over time effects. In PvP, [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259718) frees you from any stun, and with [Perception](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20600) you spot stealthed rogues earlier.

## Starting zones and class quests

- **Humans** start in Northshire, **Gnomes** in Coldridge Valley, **Undead** in Deathknell, **Orcs and Trolls** together in the Valley of Trials.
- As in Classic, there are class quests for your demons. Around **level 20** a new quest chain for Horde and Alliance is added, rewarding a Small Soul Pouch.

## How different is the lore of WoW Forever?

WoW Forever is set in the first year of World of Warcraft, after the fall of Lordaeron and before Molten Core. Blizzard calls it a **new branch** of World of Warcraft that does not exist in the same continuity as the current game. The world stays in classic Azeroth, the Dark Portal never opens, and things can happen that never happened in the known story, such as Trolls becoming apprentices of an orc warlock.

## Our tip

If you want to play the story of the Warlock, pick the **Orc**, which is also the strongest Horde PvE pick in Forever. If you play a lot of PvP, the **Undead** serves you best. On the Alliance side, decide by playstyle: **Gnome** for Destruction and burst, **Human** for Affliction and more mana through Life Tap. And if you want something completely new, start as a **Troll Warlock** with Nartok in the Valley of Trials.
