The Hunter in WoW Forever is closer to the Classic Hunter than you might think, but different in one key way: **no race is tied to a weapon anymore.** Blizzard removed all weapon racials so that dungeon and raid loot is never judged through the lens of "required" weapon types. The Dwarf no longer needs a gun, the Troll no longer needs a bow. In return there are new combinations: **Human Hunters** and **Skyborne** on both sides.

This guide is based on Blizzard's development notes from 26 and 27 October 2026, the state shortly before launch on **4 November 2026**. We will check it against the live game after launch.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="orc,nightelf,human,troll,tauren,dwarf" mode="forever" reasons="Horde: Command for your pet, strongest Beast Mastery pick|Alliance: Elune's Light for burst, Shadowmeld in PvP|Alliance: new in Forever, Ruthless and Will to Survive|Horde: Berserking for 15 seconds, Beast Slaying|Horde: War Stomp against the dead zone, Plainsrunning|Alliance: Stoneform, hit chance and Big Game Hunter"}
::

- **Horde:** **Orc** for Beast Mastery, **Troll** as a strong alternative.
- **Alliance:** **Night Elf** for burst and PvP, **Human** as the all-rounder.
- **Leveling and Survival:** according to GamerBlurb, **Skyborne** suit a Survival Hunter with steady damage.
- **Important:** almost all recommendations from the big sites predate the final changes on 26 October, and none is based on simulations.

## What changes for Hunters in Forever

- **Pets grow with your gear.** According to Blizzard, pets take their stats from your gear. Warcraft Tavern adds that they inherit your full critical strike chance. Everything that makes you stronger therefore makes your pet stronger too.
- **Marksmanship often plays without a pet.** With the Lone Wolf talent you deal 20 percent more damage without a pet. For those Hunters the Orc's Command does nothing.
- **Survival is a melee Hunter.** With the new talents you fight with two weapons in melee, so attack power counts for both.
- **No weapon racials.** Pick bow, gun or crossbow freely.

## The Alliance races

### Night Elf: the Sentinels

In the time of WoW Forever the **Sentinels** still watch over Ashenvale and Teldrassil, and a Night Elf with a bow and a nightsaber is the most classic Hunter image of all. New is [Elune's Light](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259799): 10 percent more critical strike chance for 15 seconds, every 3 minutes. Because pets inherit your critical strike chance, your companion benefits too. Blizzard left this ability untouched in the final changes, which makes it relatively stronger compared to the other races.

[Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20580) makes you invisible while standing still. In combat it drops threat, but then has a 2 minute cooldown. [Quickness](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20582) gives 1 percent dodge and 2 percent movement speed, and [Wisp Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20585) makes you faster after death.

### Human: the new Hunter from Elwynn

Human Hunters have existed in retail since Cataclysm, but they are new to classic Azeroth. You learn from **Josephine Carson in Goldshire**, who greets new students with a jab: "you don't look like you could fully draw a longbow, nor load a musket. I teach real hunters." You tame your first companions in Elwynn Forest in three short quests with a [boar](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94792), a [wolf](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94863) and a [bear](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94864).

[Ruthless](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20597) gives 1 percent critical strike (it was 2 percent during the beta), [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259718) removes stuns, and [Perception](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20600) helps against stealthed rogues. Method and Warcraft Tavern recommend the Human for the Alliance, though it lost some of its lead in the final changes.

### Dwarf: no gun anymore, but Stoneform

The mountaineers of Ironforge have been famous marksmen since the Second War. In Forever, the Dwarf's Gun Specialization is gone. Instead they get a new racial with 1 percent hit chance (Artisan's Precision, added on 26 October, with no Wowhead page yet) and [Big Game Hunter](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259721) with 5 percent more damage against beasts.

[Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20594) removes bleeds, poisons and diseases, briefly makes you immune to them and reduces physical damage taken by 10 percent. In PvP against rogues and warriors that is strong, and GamerBlurb and Warcraft Tavern recommend the Dwarf for it. [Find Treasure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2481) shows you treasure and sometimes grants extra items from chests. RankedBoost puts the Dwarf at the very bottom, though that was before the new hit chance.

### High Order Skyborne

The Skyborne start on **Zephras Isle**. Their Hunters learn from Quel'ana Quickgale in the Hall of Arms in Valanaar, who says: "The hunters and rangers of Zephras have never been more needed." You tame your first companion in [this quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94978): "Every hunter begins by learning the bond between beast and master."

[Wind Blessed](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259710) gives a permanent 2 percent haste, and [Read Ley Line](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259705) briefly raises your regeneration, at a ley line even for an hour. With [Walk on Air](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259416) you glide through the air for 10 seconds, and [Elemental Insight](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259707) increases your damage against elementals by 5 percent. You need at least the Skyborne Heroic Pack to play Skyborne.

## The Horde races

### Orc: Command for your pet

The Orcs have found a new home in Durotar, and their Hunters roam the red steppe with wolves and scorpids. Since 26 October the Orc has **Command** again: your pets deal 5 percent more damage. Because pets grow with your gear, that is stronger than in Classic and makes the Orc the clearest pick for **Beast Mastery**. For a Marksmanship Hunter with Lone Wolf, though, it does nothing. Wowhead has no page for the Forever version yet.

Add [Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20572) with 10 percent more attack power for 15 seconds, without the old healing penalty, and [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20573), which shortens stuns by 20 percent. Method recommends the Orc for the Horde, and RankedBoost puts it in the top tier.

### Troll: Berserking for 15 seconds

[Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20554) now increases your attack speed by 10 percent for **15 seconds**, much more reliable than in Classic, where the bonus depended on your missing health. [Beast Slaying](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20557) gives 5 percent more damage against beasts, very handy while leveling, and [Rapid Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260270) heals you for half your health over 6 seconds. According to search results Icy Veins names the Troll as the best Horde pick, and RankedBoost puts it level with the Orc.

### Tauren: War Stomp against the dead zone

The Tauren have hunted the plains of **Mulgore** for generations. In Forever the Hunter has an 8-yard dead zone in which you cannot shoot. [War Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20549) is ideal for exactly that: it stuns up to five enemies around you for 2 seconds, and its cooldown drops to 90 seconds. New is [Plainsrunning](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259918): every 5 seconds of movement makes you 2 percent faster, up to 60 percent. [Endurance](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20550) only gives 5 percent more health at launch; its damage bonuses were removed on 26 October.

### Skyborne: the Windshapers

The Windshapers descend from the first shen'dorei to receive skysight and are sent to find their vanished wind spirits. Instead of Read Ley Line they get [Skysight](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259686): 15 percent more run and mounted speed for 15 seconds, and for an hour if used at an Elemental Convergence. They share Wind Blessed, Walk on Air and Elemental Insight with the High Order.

## How different is the lore of WoW Forever?

WoW Forever is set in the first year of World of Warcraft, after the fall of Lordaeron and before Molten Core. Blizzard calls it a **new branch** of World of Warcraft that does not exist in the same continuity as the current game. Human Hunters in Goldshire and Skyborne taming their first companions on Zephras are among the things that never existed in classic Azeroth.

## Our tip

For the strongest Beast Mastery Hunter at launch, pick the **Orc**. On the Alliance side the **Night Elf** with Elune's Light is the most exciting choice, and the **Human** the uncomplicated all-rounder. If you want something completely new, start as a **Skyborne** on Zephras, and if you want to lead your companion on long journeys across the plains, you will be happy with the **Tauren** and Plainsrunning.
