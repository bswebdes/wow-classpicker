## What are class colors used for?

- **Discord roles**: guilds often give members a role in their class color. You can paste the hex code straight into Discord.
- **Addons and UI**: in-game colors are given as values between 0 and 1. The last column of the table is meant for that.
- **Graphics and streams**: overlays, thumbnails and guild logos look more cohesive in the official colors.

## Good to know

The class colors have been tweaked slightly over the years, so you will sometimes find slightly different values online, for example for the Paladin or Shaman. The values above match the current version of the game.
