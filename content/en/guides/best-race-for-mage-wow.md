Good news first: In WoW Midnight **all 26 races can play Mage**, and none of them will hold you back. The damage difference is smaller than most people think. So this guide covers both sides: the honest numbers for everyone who wants the last percent, and the question of which race actually feels right as a Mage, from lore to racial abilities to looks.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="voidelf,dwarf,troll,tauren,bloodelf,human" reasons="Alliance all-rounder: strong Arcane, no spell pushback, teleport|Alliance: best race for Fire and Frost, Stoneform for M+|Horde: best race for Arcane|Horde: best race for Fire and Frost|Horde: the lore pick, magisters of Silvermoon|Alliance: the lore pick, heir of Dalaran"}
::

- **Numbers only:** Alliance **Dwarf** (Fire, Frost) or **Void Elf** (Arcane), Horde **Troll** (Arcane) or **Tauren** (Fire, Frost). The neutral **Pandaren** sits near the top in every spec.
- **Mythic+:** **Dwarf** for [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594). Among top players, about three out of four Arcane Mages are Dwarves.
- **PvP:** Alliance **Night Elf** ([Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984)), Horde **Highmountain Tauren** or **Orc**.
- **Lore and feel:** **Human**, **Blood Elf**, **Nightborne**, **Gnome**, **Void Elf**.

## How much does your race really matter?

Not much. In the [Bloodmallet](https://bloodmallet.com/chart/mage/fire/races/castingpatchwerk) simulations (28 October 2026, Season 2) the best race is only **1.5 to 2.6 percent** ahead of the weakest, depending on spec. Most races are less than **1 percent** apart. For comparison: a few item levels or one cleanly played burst window give you more.

| Spec | Best race (Alliance) | Best race (Horde) | Gap best to weakest race |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Arcane](/en/classes/mage/arcane) | Void Elf | Troll | 1.5% |
| [Fire](/en/classes/mage/fire) | Dwarf | Tauren | 2.6% |
| [Frost](/en/classes/mage/frost) | Dwarf | Tauren | 1.6% |

At the bottom of every list you find Undead, Vulpera, Lightforged Draenei and Earthen. Even they are only about 2 percent behind the top. If you love your race, play it, with a clear conscience.

## The Alliance races

### Human: heir of Dalaran

Without Humans there would be no Mages as we know them. The High Elves taught them magic during the Troll Wars, and the Humans went on to found **Dalaran** and the **Kirin Tor**. Antonidas, Khadgar and Medivh, the last Guardian of Tirisfal, are all Human. So is Jaina Proudmoore, the most powerful living human sorceress, born in Kul Tiras.

[The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20598) gives you 2 percent more of all secondary stats, a solid bonus for every spec. [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59752) breaks stuns in PvP. Human is never the very best, but always a good choice. In Arcane it is almost level with the top.

### Dwarf: the numbers king

In the lore, Dwarves are craftsmen rather than arcanists, and only a few become Mages. In the numbers they lead anyway: [Might of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59224) makes critical strikes deal 204 instead of 200 percent damage, a perfect fit for Fire and Frost. On top of that comes [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594), which removes poison, disease, curse, magic and bleed effects and reduces physical damage taken. In Mythic+ that is worth its weight in gold, which is why most top players there play Dwarf.

### Gnome: the arcane genius

Gnomes are known as **remarkably gifted arcanists** and have always been part of the Kirin Tor. Indus, one of the founders of the Council of Tirisfal, was a Gnome, and Millhouse Manastorm is the best known (and loudest) Gnome Mage. [Nimble Fingers](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=92680) gives 1 percent haste, [Expansive Mind](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20591) 5 percent more mana, and [Escape Artist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20589) frees you from roots and snares. Gnomes sit in the middle of the simulations, but hardly any race looks this determined while casting.

### Night Elf: the PvP favorite

For Night Elves, magic was taboo for a long time after the War of the Ancients. Only after the Cataclysm were the Highborne of the Shen'dralar welcomed back. A Night Elf Mage therefore has a great story to tell. In gameplay, [Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984) is what counts: it lets you dodge projectiles and crowd control in PvP and skip some mechanics in dungeons. In the 3v3 ladder every second Frost Mage is a Night Elf. [Touch of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154748) adds 1 percent haste at night or 1 percent critical strike by day.

### Void Elf: the all-rounder

Void Elves were Blood Elves who experimented with the Void under Magister Umbric until Silvermoon banished them. Their kit is almost perfect for casters: [Entropic Embrace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255669) regularly boosts your damage by 5 percent, [Preternatural Calm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255670) stops spell pushback when you are hit, and [Spatial Rift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=256948) lets you teleport. In Arcane the Void Elf ties with the Troll for first place, and in PvP it is the second choice after the Night Elf.

### Draenei and Lightforged Draenei

The Draenei descend from the eredar, whose arcane tradition is older than that of the Humans. A Draenei Mage fits well, even though the race is better known for Priests and Paladins. [Heroic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6562) adds Intellect, and [Gift of the Naaru](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59548) heals you for 20 percent of your health over 5 seconds.

Mages are rare among the **Lightforged**, with Archmage Y'mera the best known. Their attack [Light's Judgment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255647) is weak, which puts them near the bottom of every simulation. If you love the glowing look, you only lose about 2 percent.

### Worgen

Gilneas had its own tradition of magic before the curse struck the city. A Worgen Mage who switches between human and wolf form is a great roleplay character. [Viciousness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68975) gives 1 percent critical strike, [Darkflight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68992) makes you 40 percent faster for 10 seconds. Middle of the pack in the simulations.

### Dark Iron Dwarf

The Dark Iron Dwarves have a long history of dark magic beneath Blackrock. [Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221) removes harmful effects and briefly raises your primary stat, a small burst and a cleanse in one. The [Mole Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265225) is also one of the most fun racials in the game.

### Kul Tiran

Mages are rare among the Kul Tirans, a seafaring nation that puts its faith in its tidesages. Jaina Proudmoore is the big exception. In gameplay the racials are small: [Brush It Off](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=291628) gives 1 percent versatility, [Haymaker](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=287712) is a punch with a stun.

### Mechagnome

The lore says little about Mechagnome magic, but their [Combat Analysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=312923) builds up more and more primary stat in long fights. Method recommends Mechagnome for raids as Arcane and Frost, and in the simulations it sits just behind the top.

## The Horde races

### Blood Elf: the magisters of Silvermoon

No race is as closely tied to arcane magic as the Blood Elves. The **magisters of Silvermoon**, Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider, Grand Magister Rommath and Aethas Sunreaver have shaped the history of magic for millennia. And Midnight is set in **Quel'Thalas**: as a Blood Elf Mage walking through Eversong, you are home.

[Arcane Acuity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154742) gives 1 percent critical strike, [Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=28730) dispels a buff from nearby enemies and restores mana. In the simulations the Blood Elf sits in the middle, for the story it is the first choice.

### Nightborne: heirs of the Nightwell

The Nightborne of Suramar lived off the arcane power of the Nightwell for ten thousand years, and among them literally anyone can cast small spells. Grand Magistrix Elisande and First Arcanist Thalyssra stand for this race. [Magical Affinity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255665) increases your magic damage by 1 percent, and [Arcane Pulse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=260364) slows enemies around you. Ideal on paper, but only mid-pack in the simulations.

### Troll: best Horde Arcane Mage

Trolls like to mix arcane power with shadow magic and voodoo, and the Zandalari illusionists are famous for it. In gameplay, [Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=26297) is the reason for their top spot: 10 percent haste for 12 seconds, perfectly suited to the Arcane Mage's big burst windows. [Da Voodoo Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58943) also shortens snares.

### Tauren: best Horde Fire and Frost Mage

Tauren Mages only exist since Dragonflight. In their lore they are **runemasters** who see the ley lines of the world as the veins of the Earth Mother, a lovely and unique take on magic. In gameplay, [Brawn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154743) is the Horde version of Might of the Mountain (critical strikes for 204 percent), and [War Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20549) adds an area stun.

### Highmountain Tauren

Their strength is PvP: [Rugged Tenacity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255659) reduces every hit you take by a fixed amount, and [Bull Rush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255654) charges forward and knocks enemies down. That makes them the first PvP pick for the Horde.

### Orc

Orcs could not become Mages at all before the Cataclysm, so the Arcweavers of the True Horde are a fairly new chapter. [Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33702) raises your Intellect for 15 seconds, and [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20573) shortens stuns. The second PvP pick for the Horde.

### Undead

The Forsaken kept the magical skill of their former lives, and Meryl Felstorm is an undead sorcerer of the old guard. In the numbers, Undead are at the very bottom, but in PvP [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7744) against fear and sleep is still useful.

### Goblin, Mag'har, Zandalari and Vulpera

- **Goblin:** magic as a business. [Time is Money](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69042) gives 1 percent haste, [Rocket Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69041) a little extra damage. Middle of the pack.
- **Mag'har Orc:** their Mages learned from the ogres of Highmaul. [Ancestral Call](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274738) briefly boosts one of your best secondary stats. Lower middle.
- **Zandalari Troll:** for [Embrace of the Loa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292492), definitely pick [Pa'ku](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292463). The other loa are far behind in the simulations.
- **Vulpera:** the lore says nothing about Vulpera Mages, and [Bag of Tricks](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=312411) is not enough to reach the top. For charm, though, they beat every other race.

## The neutral races

- **Pandaren:** the Omnia Mages of the Shado-Pan are the lore foundation. [Epicurean](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107072) doubles the stats from food, which keeps the Pandaren among the top six in **every** spec.
- **Dracthyr:** they carry the arcane magic of the blue dragonflight, and have been able to play Mage since The War Within. [Awakened](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=365575) gives mastery, and with [Soar](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=369536) you travel the open world faster than any other race.
- **Earthen:** they cannot eat and consume gems instead ([Ingest Minerals](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436341)). The weakest race in the simulations, but still tempting for fans of the titans.
- **Haranir:** the new race of Midnight. [Lash Out](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238623) increases your critical damage by 1 percent, [Thorn Bloom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1237885) hits up to eight enemies. About 1 percent behind the top.

## Our tip

Pick the race whose Mage you picture when you close your eyes. A Blood Elf in the halls of Silvermoon, a Gnome with a hat that is too big, a Tauren runemaster under the stars of Mulgore. You will make up the 1 to 2 percent with gear faster than you will recover from starting over with a race you never liked.
