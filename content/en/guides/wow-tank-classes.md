Tanks are the backbone of every group: they draw the enemies' attention, set the pace in dungeons and hold the boss in place in raids. In return, a tank almost always finds a group right away. Six classes can tank in WoW, and each one feels different.

## Which tank suits you?

**For beginners:** the **Blood Death Knight** and the **Protection Paladin** are considered especially forgiving. Both have strong self-healing and plenty of panic buttons.

**For mobility:** **Vengeance Demon Hunter** and **Brewmaster** move fast and are great for the pace of Mythic+.

**For classic fans:** the **Protection Warrior** is the tank many remember from the old days: shield up, gather the pack, hold the line.

**For flexibility:** the **Guardian Druid** can shift into other forms between fights and is at home in every role.

## Tips for new tanks

1. **Learn the dungeons.** As a tank you lead the group, and knowing routes and packs matters more than a perfect rotation.
2. **Use your defensives actively.** Many tank deaths happen because defensive abilities are pressed too late or not at all.
3. **Stay calm.** A tank who communicates clearly makes every group better.
