Hardly any class leaves as much room for your own imagination as the Hunter. You don't just pick a race, you also pick your companion, your bow or gun and the wilderness you come from. In WoW Midnight **all 26 races** can play Hunter, and each has its own first companion. The damage difference is small. This guide covers the honest numbers and the stories that belong to each race.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="dwarf,nightelf,tauren,troll,bloodelf,mechagnome" reasons="Alliance: Stoneform, Mythic+ favorite, strong Marksmanship|Alliance: Shadowmeld, first PvP pick, Sentinel lore|Horde: strong in all three specs, best race for Marksmanship|Horde: best race for Survival|Horde: the rangers of Quel'Thalas|Alliance: best race for single-target Beast Mastery"}
::

- **Numbers only:** [Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) **Mechagnome**, **Human** or **Pandaren**, Horde **Tauren** or **Orc** · [Marksmanship](/en/classes/hunter/marksmanship) **Dwarf** and **Tauren** · [Survival](/en/classes/hunter/survival) **Troll**, Alliance **Human** or **Dwarf**.
- **Mythic+:** **Dwarf** for [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594), the most common race among top players in all three specs.
- **PvP:** Alliance **Night Elf**, Horde **Highmountain Tauren**, and **Orc** for Beast Mastery.
- **Lore:** **Night Elf**, **Blood Elf**, **Dwarf**, **Tauren**.

## How much does your race really matter?

Even less than for other classes. In the [Bloodmallet](https://bloodmallet.com/chart/hunter/beast_mastery/races/castingpatchwerk) simulations (28 October 2026) the best race is only **1.3 percent** ahead of the weakest for Beast Mastery, **1.8 percent** for Survival and **2.4 percent** for Marksmanship.

| Spec | Best Alliance pick | Best Horde pick | Gap best to weakest race |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) | Mechagnome, Human | Tauren, Orc | 1.3% |
| [Marksmanship](/en/classes/hunter/marksmanship) | Dwarf | Tauren | 2.4% |
| [Survival](/en/classes/hunter/survival) | Human, Dwarf | Troll, Tauren | 1.8% |

Undead, Nightborne and Lightforged sit at the bottom, and even they are only about 1.3 to 2.4 percent behind the top.

## The Alliance races

### Night Elf: the Sentinels

Since the War of the Ancients, the **Sentinels** have watched over the forests of Kalimdor under General Shandris Feathermoon. Hardly any image fits the Hunter better than a Night Elf with a bow and a nightsaber in the moonlight.

In gameplay Night Elves are the first Alliance PvP pick: [Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984) lets you dodge projectiles and crowd control, and in Mythic+ it lets you skip packs. [Touch of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154748) gives 1 percent haste at night or 1 percent critical strike by day, and [Quickness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20582) makes you faster. Almost every second Survival Hunter in the 3v3 ladder is a Night Elf.

### Dwarf: guns and the greatest hunter of Azeroth

**Hemet Nesingwary**, "the greatest hunter on Azeroth", is a Dwarf. He ran the Nesingwary Safari and wrote *The Green Hills of Stranglethorn*. Dwarf Hunters traditionally carry a gun instead of a bow and start with a bear as their companion.

In the numbers Dwarves are strong everywhere: [Might of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59224) makes critical strikes deal 204 instead of 200 percent damage, which makes the Dwarf the best Alliance race for Marksmanship. The real reason for their popularity, though, is [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594), which removes poison, disease, curse, magic and bleed effects. More than two thirds of the top Beast Mastery Hunters in Mythic+ are Dwarves.

### Human and Mechagnome: the Beast Mastery top

- **Human:** [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20598) gives 2 percent to all secondary stats and puts the Human high up in every spec; for Survival it is the best Alliance race. In PvP, [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59752) breaks stuns. Tyrathan Khort of the Unseen Path is one of the best known human hunters.
- **Mechagnome:** [Combat Analysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=312923) builds up more and more Agility in combat and makes the Mechagnome the best race for single-target Beast Mastery. A Hunter with a self-built mecha pet fits perfectly.

### Draenei, Lightforged, Worgen, Void Elf, Gnome, Dark Iron, Kul Tiran

- **Draenei:** [Gift of the Naaru](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59543) heals you, [Heroic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6562) adds Agility.
- **Lightforged Draenei:** [Light's Judgment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255647) is weak, at the bottom for Marksmanship and Survival.
- **Worgen:** [Viciousness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68975) gives 1 percent critical strike, [Darkflight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68992) makes you faster. A Worgen taming the beast within while leading another at their side is a lovely character.
- **Void Elf:** Void Elves are former rangers of Quel'Thalas, and Alleria Windrunner was the first of them. [Entropic Embrace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255669) regularly boosts your damage.
- **Gnome:** [Nimble Fingers](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=92680) gives 1 percent haste. A Gnome with a gun and a wolf has style.
- **Dark Iron Dwarf:** [Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221) cleanses you and briefly raises your Agility.
- **Kul Tiran:** [Brush It Off](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=291628) gives 1 percent versatility. A sailor with a parrot companion, why not?

## The Horde races

### Blood Elf: the rangers of Quel'Thalas

The rangers of Quel'Thalas were the finest archers of Azeroth. **Sylvanas Windrunner** was their Ranger-General before Arthas killed her. **Alleria Windrunner**, Ranger-Captain of Quel'Thalas, is now a Void Elf and the main protagonist of Midnight. **Vereesa Windrunner** founded the Silver Covenant and leads a refuge of her own in Quel'Thalas in Midnight. As a Blood Elf Hunter you belong to this tradition, and Midnight brings it home.

The Hunter gets its own version of [Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=80483): it dispels a buff from enemies and gives you 15 Focus. Add [Arcane Acuity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154742) with 1 percent critical strike. In Mythic+ the Blood Elf is the second most common race for Survival.

### Tauren and Highmountain Tauren

The Tauren have hunted the plains of Mulgore for generations. **Huln Highmountain** led the tauren into battle as far back as the War of the Ancients and is the ancestor of the Highmountain Tauren. Like Dwarves, Tauren Hunters start with a gun.

- **Tauren:** [Brawn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154743) makes critical strikes deal 204 instead of 200 percent damage. That makes the Tauren the best race for **Marksmanship** and strong in all three specs. [War Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20549) stuns enemies around you.
- **Highmountain Tauren:** [Rugged Tenacity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255659) reduces every hit by a fixed amount, and [Bull Rush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255654) knocks enemies down. The first Horde PvP pick, almost every second Marksmanship Hunter in the 3v3 ladder.

### Orc: Rexxar and the beastmasters

**Rexxar**, half orc and half ogre of the Mok'nathal, roamed Kalimdor with his bear Misha until Thrall named him Champion of the Horde. No other character embodies the beastmaster quite like him.

[Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=21563) increases your pets' damage, and Method calls the Orc strong for **Beast Mastery** especially on many targets. [Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20572) gives you more attack power for 15 seconds, and [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20573) shortens stuns. In the 3v3 ladder the Orc is the most common race for Beast Mastery.

### Troll: best Survival Hunter

Trolls are born hunters, and the jungles and islands of Azeroth are their hunting grounds. [Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=26297) gives 10 percent haste for 12 seconds and makes the Troll the best race for **Survival**, on one target and on many.

### Undead

Nathanos Blightcaller was the only human Ranger Lord and later became Forsaken. In the numbers, though, Undead Hunters sit at the bottom in all three specs. Their strength remains [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7744) against fear in PvP.

### Goblin, Nightborne, Mag'har, Zandalari and Vulpera

- **Goblin:** [Time is Money](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69042) gives 1 percent haste, [Rocket Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69041) extra damage. A Goblin with a rocket rifle, of course.
- **Nightborne:** the weakest race for Beast Mastery; the magic of the Nightwell barely helps a Hunter.
- **Mag'har Orc:** [Ancestral Call](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274738) briefly boosts one of your best secondary stats, and [Sympathetic Vigor](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=273217) gives your pet more health.
- **Zandalari Troll:** pick [Embrace of Pa'ku](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292361) for damage; [Embrace of Kimbul](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292363) is the Hunter version with bleed damage.
- **Vulpera:** desert foxes with a bow and an alpaca, need we say more.

## The neutral races

- **Pandaren:** [Epicurean](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107072) doubles the stats from food, and on multiple targets the Pandaren is the best race for Beast Mastery.
- **Dracthyr:** Hunters since The War Within, starting with a Vorquin as companion. [Wing Buffet](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=357214) knocks enemies back, and [Soar](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=369536) makes you fast in the open world.
- **Earthen:** they start with a Proto Ram. [Azerite Surge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436344) is an area attack, and [Ingest Minerals](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436341) replaces food with gems.
- **Haranir:** the new race of Midnight starts with a saptor. [Thorn Bloom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1237885) hits up to eight enemies, and [Lash Out](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238623) increases your critical damage. About 0.6 to 0.9 percent behind the top, and Method expects the Haranir to get stronger later in the expansion.

## Our tip

Picture your Hunter with their companion: a Night Elf with a nightsaber, a Dwarf with a gun and a bear, a Tauren with an eagle over the plains, a Vulpera with a snake in their pack. The picture that makes you smile is the right race. If you play high Mythic+, the Dwarf is worth a look, and in PvP the Night Elf or Highmountain Tauren.
