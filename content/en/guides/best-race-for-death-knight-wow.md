Every Death Knight has a second story: the story of their death. Before you drew your runeblade you were a hero of your people, then the Lich King brought you back. The race you choose decides who you were before you died. In WoW Midnight **23 races** can play Death Knight, and the damage difference is smaller than most people think. This guide covers both: the honest numbers and the stories that fit each race.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="human,dwarf,troll,nightelf,orc,highmountain" reasons="Alliance: best race for Frost, the race of the great Death Knights|Alliance: first pick for the Blood tank, Stoneform|Horde: best race for Unholy|Alliance: popular in Mythic+, Shadowmeld and dodge|Horde: PvP favorite for Frost, Command for your ghoul|Horde: PvP favorite for Unholy"}
::

- **Numbers only:** [Frost](/en/classes/death-knight/frost) **Human** (Alliance) or **Zandalari with Pa'ku** and **Tauren** (Horde), [Unholy](/en/classes/death-knight/unholy) **Troll** (Horde) or **Human** and **Dwarf** (Alliance).
- **Tank ([Blood](/en/classes/death-knight/blood)):** **Dwarf** for [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594), then **Night Elf**.
- **Mythic+:** **Night Elf** and **Dwarf** lead for Frost and Unholy.
- **PvP:** Horde **Orc** (Frost) and **Highmountain Tauren** (Unholy), Alliance **Pandaren**, **Human** or **Dark Iron Dwarf**.
- **Lore:** **Human**, **Blood Elf**, **Orc**, **Undead**.

## How much does your race really matter?

Not much. In the [Bloodmallet](https://bloodmallet.com/chart/death_knight/frost/races/castingpatchwerk) simulations (28 October 2026) the best race is **2.4 percent** ahead of the weakest for Frost and **2 percent** for Unholy. Most races are less than 1 percent apart. There are no damage sims for the Blood tank, where survival tools matter, and Method explicitly calls the difference "very minimal".

| Spec | Best Alliance pick | Best Horde pick | Gap best to weakest race |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Frost](/en/classes/death-knight/frost) | Human | Zandalari (Pa'ku), Tauren | 2.4% |
| [Unholy](/en/classes/death-knight/unholy) | Human, Dwarf | Troll, Orc | 2.0% |
| [Blood](/en/classes/death-knight/blood) (tank) | Dwarf, Night Elf | no clear favorite | – |

Vulpera and Lightforged sit at the bottom, only about 2.4 percent behind the top.

## Where Death Knights come from

In the Third War the Lich King created the first Death Knights from fallen paladins and warriors, and the first and greatest of them was Arthas. From **Acherus: The Ebon Hold** above the Scarlet Enclave they rode to war under Highlord Darion Mograine. At the Second Battle for **Light's Hope Chapel**, the Lich King sacrificed his own knights in an attempt to kill Tirion Fordring. The survivors broke free and founded the **Knights of the Ebon Blade**.

The classic races start as Death Knights at level 8 in Acherus. The allied races and Pandaren were added with Shadowlands and begin at level 10 in Icecrown Citadel.

## The Alliance races

### Human: the race of the great Death Knights

Almost all of the great Death Knights were Human. **Arthas Menethil**, the prince of Lordaeron who became the Lich King. **Darion Mograine**, who founded the Knights of the Ebon Blade and leads the Four Horsemen. **Thassarian**, the first Death Knight to rejoin the Alliance. And **Bolvar Fordragon**, who wore the Lich King's crown until the Helm was destroyed and today leads the Ebon Blade as Highlord. As a Human Death Knight you carry that story forward.

It fits in gameplay too: [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20598) gives 2 percent to all secondary stats and makes the Human the best race for **Frost** and strong in Unholy. In PvP, [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59752) breaks stuns.

### Dwarf: the tank king

For a **Blood Death Knight** there is hardly anything better than [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20594): it removes poison, disease, curse, magic and bleed effects and reduces physical damage taken by 10 percent for 8 seconds. More than every second top Blood tank in Mythic+ is a Dwarf. Add [Might of the Mountain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59224) with stronger critical strikes, which also puts the Dwarf high up for Unholy.

### Night Elf: shadows between the trees

A Night Elf raised by the Scourge is an especially tragic figure, since the Night Elves only lost their immortality late in their history. In gameplay, Night Elves are popular in Mythic+: [Quickness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20582) adds dodge and movement speed, and [Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984) lets you skip packs. Night Elf is the most common race among top Frost Death Knights in Mythic+ and the second pick for the Blood tank.

### Dark Iron Dwarf

Lady Tamakeen was the first known Dark Iron Death Knight. [Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221) removes harmful effects like bleeds and briefly raises your Strength. That makes the Dark Iron Dwarf the first Alliance PvP pick for **Unholy**, and [Forged in Flames](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265224) also reduces physical damage taken.

### Draenei, Lightforged, Worgen, Void Elf, Gnome, Kul Tiran, Mechagnome

- **Draenei:** [Gift of the Naaru](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59545) heals you, [Heroic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6562) adds Strength. A fallen servant of the naaru as a Death Knight is a powerful story.
- **Lightforged Draenei:** a being of Light turned servant of death may be the biggest contradiction in the game. At the bottom of the numbers.
- **Worgen:** [Viciousness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=68975) gives 1 percent critical strike. A Worgen Death Knight carries two curses at once.
- **Void Elf:** [Entropic Embrace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255669) regularly boosts your damage. Middle of the pack.
- **Gnome:** [Nimble Fingers](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=92680) gives 1 percent haste, and Method recommends the Gnome for Unholy. A Gnome with a giant runeblade is simply wonderful.
- **Kul Tiran:** [Brush It Off](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=291628) gives versatility and heals you a little, good for tanks.
- **Mechagnome:** [Combat Analysis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=312923) builds up more and more Strength in combat.

## The Horde races

### Blood Elf: Koltira and the Horde

**Koltira Deathweaver** was Thassarian's closest friend and the first Death Knight to rejoin the Horde. His story between the factions is one of the most tragic of the class. Because Midnight is set in Quel'Thalas, a Blood Elf Death Knight returns to the places of their former life.

In gameplay the Death Knight gets its own version of [Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50613): it dispels a buff from nearby enemies and gives you 20 Runic Power. [Arcane Acuity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154742) adds 1 percent critical strike, which puts the Blood Elf high up for Frost on multiple targets.

### Orc: Nazgrim's fate

Nazgrim served the Horde faithfully as a warrior until he fell at the Siege of Orgrimmar. In Legion he was raised as the first new Horseman of the Ebon Blade, a bitter turn for a soldier who only ever wanted to serve his faction.

[Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20572) raises your attack power for 15 seconds, [Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=21563) strengthens the ghoul and other undead of the **Unholy Death Knight**, and [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20573) shortens stuns by 20 percent. In PvP the Orc is the first Horde pick for Frost.

### Undead: freed twice

The Forsaken and the Death Knights share the same origin: the Scourge. An Undead Death Knight has broken free of the Lich King's grip twice. In PvP, [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7744) against fear and sleep remains useful.

### Troll: best Unholy Death Knight

[Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=26297) gives you 10 percent haste for 12 seconds and makes the Troll the best race for **Unholy**, on one target and on many. The lore fits as well: hardly any race knows more about spirits, voodoo and death.

### Tauren and Highmountain Tauren

- **Tauren:** [Brawn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=154743) makes critical strikes deal 204 instead of 200 percent damage and makes the Tauren the second-best Horde pick for Frost. [Endurance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20550) adds Stamina, and [War Stomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20549) stuns enemies around you.
- **Highmountain Tauren:** [Rugged Tenacity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255659) reduces every hit by a fixed amount, ideal for tanks and PvP. More than half of all Unholy Death Knights in the 3v3 ladder are Highmountain Tauren.

### Zandalari, Goblin, Nightborne, Mag'har and Vulpera

- **Zandalari Troll:** with [Embrace of Pa'ku](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292361) the best Horde race for Frost. As a tank, [Embrace of Krag'wa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292364) with extra health and armor is worth it.
- **Goblin:** [Rocket Barrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69041) and a touch of madness. Middle of the pack.
- **Nightborne:** a child of the Nightwell turned Death Knight. [Magical Affinity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255665) helps the Death Knight's frost and shadow damage.
- **Mag'har Orc:** [Ancestral Call](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274738) briefly boosts one of your best secondary stats, and Method recommends it for Frost.
- **Vulpera:** at the bottom of the numbers, but an undead desert fox with a runeblade is unforgettable.

## Pandaren

Gravewalker Gie was the first known Pandaren Death Knight. In gameplay the Pandaren is a strong pick: [Epicurean](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107072) doubles the stats from food, and according to Skill-Capped, [Quaking Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107079) is the best Alliance racial for Frost in PvP.

## Our tip

Think about who your Death Knight was before they died. A knight from Lordaeron, a Dwarf from Ironforge, a ranger from Quel'Thalas, a Tauren warrior from Mulgore. That story carries you much further than 2 percent of damage. If you tank Blood in high Mythic+, the Dwarf is worth a look.
