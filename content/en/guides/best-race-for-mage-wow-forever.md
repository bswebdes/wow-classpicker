WoW Forever brings the Mage back to classic Azeroth, but not quite the way you remember it from WoW Classic. Blizzard redesigned the racial abilities with the goal of giving every race **its own identity** again. On top of that come two new combinations: **Orc Mages** and the **High Order Skyborne**.

This guide is based on Blizzard's development notes from 26 and 27 October 2026, the state shortly before launch on **4 November 2026**. We will check it against the live game after launch.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="gnome,human,skyborne-alliance,undead,troll,orc" mode="forever" reasons="Alliance: strongest pick, Eureka! for burst and mana costs|Alliance: solid, Will to Survive against stuns|Alliance: new, best race for leveling, permanent haste|Horde: strong in PvP, Cannibalize restores mana too|Horde: Berserking for burst, fast regeneration|Horde: new in Forever, Blood Fury for spell power"}
::

- **Alliance:** **Gnome** is the pick of almost every big site. **Human** and **Skyborne** follow closely.
- **Horde:** the sites disagree. **Undead** for PvP, **Troll** for burst, **Orc** as the new combination.
- **Leveling:** **Skyborne** and **Undead** save you drinking breaks.
- **Important:** none of these recommendations is based on simulations. The differences are about **1 percent**.

## The Alliance races

### Gnome: the new Eureka!

Gnomes have always been among the most gifted arcanists of Azeroth, and Gnomeregan was a city of inventions and magic. In WoW Forever they get an ability that fits them perfectly: [Eureka!](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259817) lowers the cost of your spells by 10 percent and increases your damage by 10 percent for 8 seconds, every 2 minutes. Since 1 October, Eureka! no longer affects periodic damage, but for a Mage with direct spells like Fireball, Frostbolt and Arcane Missiles that is hardly a loss.

On top of that come [Expansive Mind](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20591) with 5 percent more mana and [Escape Artist](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20589), which frees you from roots and snares and then makes you immune to them for 3 seconds. For a Mage who needs to keep distance, that is huge in PvP. No wonder Icy Veins, Mein-MMO, Timesaver and RankedBoost all put the Gnome first for the Alliance.

### Human: the all-rounder from Stormwind

Humans founded Dalaran and the Kirin Tor. In WoW Forever, Dalaran is being rebuilt, and the Kirin Tor lowered their shield earlier than expected to ally with the High Order of the Skyborne. A Human Mage stands right in the middle of this new story.

In gameplay the Human racials are broad: [Ruthless](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20597) gives 1 percent critical strike, and [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20598) 10 percent more Spirit for better mana regeneration. New is [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259718), which removes every stun every 3 minutes, worth gold against rogues in PvP. [Perception](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20600) helps you spot stealthed enemies earlier.

### High Order Skyborne: the new Mages

The Skyborne are the new race of WoW Forever. According to Blizzard, exiled Highborne forged a pact with the spirits of the wind thousands of years ago and built a civilization of their own in **Skywall**. The **High Order** descends from the Highborne scholars of Eldre'Thalas and wants to recover lost arcane knowledge so their people no longer have to rely on others. One of the first quests puts it plainly: "The power of the arcane has waned amongst our people." A Skyborne Mage is exactly what this race is fighting for. More in our guide to [the Skyborne in WoW Forever](/en/guides/skyborne-wow-forever).

Their racials make them the most comfortable race for leveling: [Read Ley Line](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259705) increases your health and mana regeneration by 50 percent for 15 seconds. Use it at a ley line and you even get 100 percent more regeneration for an hour, which ends as soon as you enter a raid. [Wind Blessed](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259710) gives a permanent 2 percent haste, the only race in Forever with one. [Walk on Air](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259416) lets you glide through the air for 10 seconds, and [Elemental Insight](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259707) increases your damage against elementals by 5 percent.

One catch: to play Skyborne you need at least the **Skyborne Heroic Pack**.

## The Horde races

### Undead: the PvP favorite

In WoW Forever the Forsaken have only just freed themselves from the Lich King and want to overthrow his rule. Many of them kept the magical skill of their former lives. [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=7744) frees you from charm, fear and sleep every 2 minutes, which makes the Undead the first PvP pick for the Horde. The lasting immunity from WoW Classic is gone, though.

For leveling, [Cannibalize](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20577) is much better now: in Forever it restores 7 percent of your health **and mana** every 2 seconds for 10 seconds. New is [Touch of the Grave](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260201), which has a 30 percent chance to drain life from your target when you cast.

### Troll: burst and regeneration

Trolls like to mix magic with voodoo and mojo, and in WoW Forever they are even the teachers of the new Orc Mages. [Berserking](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20554) increases your casting speed by 10 percent for 15 seconds, much more reliable than in WoW Classic, where the bonus depended on your missing health. New is [Rapid Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260270), which restores half your health over 6 seconds as long as you don't move or take damage. Add [Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20555) and [Beast Slaying](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20557) with 5 percent more damage against beasts, very handy while leveling.

### Orc: the new combination

There were never Orc Mages in the classic world. In WoW Forever they learn from the **troll mage Mai'ah** in the Valley of Trials, who tells you in your [first class quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98576): "Ya got da power to touch da strands of da cosmos." Blizzard has not published a long backstory for it, but the idea is clear: the trolls share their knowledge with their allies.

[Blood Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20572) increases your spell power by 10 percent for 15 seconds, every 2 minutes. The healing penalty from WoW Classic is gone. [Shatter Curse](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1299026) removes curses and briefly reduces magic damage taken, and with [Hardiness](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20573) stuns on you last 20 percent shorter. The sites disagree: Icy Veins and Mein-MMO recommend the Orc, Timesaver considers it the weakest Horde Mage.

## Starting zones and class quests

- **Humans** start in Northshire, **Gnomes** in Coldridge Valley, **Undead** in Deathknell, **Orcs and Trolls** together in the Valley of Trials.
- **Skyborne** start on the new **Zephras Isle** (levels 1 to 12) with their own Mage quests, starting with [A Student of the Arcane](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92481). At the end of the chain you earn a wand of your own.
- **Teleports** are learned at level 20 (Stormwind and Ironforge, or Orgrimmar and Undercity) and level 30 (Darnassus, or Thunder Bluff), from dedicated portal trainers in each city.

## How different is the lore of WoW Forever?

WoW Forever is set in the first year of World of Warcraft, after the fall of Lordaeron and before Molten Core. Blizzard calls it a **new branch** of World of Warcraft that does not exist in the same continuity as the current game. The developers speak of a kind of time bubble: the world stays in classic Azeroth, the Dark Portal never opens, and things can happen that never happened in the known story. A Dalaran allying with the High Order and Orcs learning magic from Trolls are exactly that.

## Our tip

If you simply want the strongest Mage, pick the Gnome on the Alliance side. If you want something new, the High Order Skyborne is the most exciting choice, with a fresh starting island, lore of its own and the most comfortable leveling. For the Horde, the Orc Mage is the combination Classic players could only dream of. And if you play a lot of PvP, you can never go wrong with the Undead.
