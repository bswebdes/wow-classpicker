No group gets far in WoW Forever without a healer. Healing is about awareness and mana: if you heal efficiently and think ahead, you will last through long fights. In WoW Forever, four classes can heal, and each feels different.

> **Our basis:** WoW Forever launches on November 4, 2026 and builds on WoW Classic. This guide is based on Classic experience. Forever reworks the talent trees with new milestone talents, so the balance between the healers may shift. We will check after launch.

::

## Priest: the all-rounder

The [Priest](/en/forever/classes/priest) has the biggest healing toolkit: [**Greater Heal**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2060){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} for the tank, [**Flash Heal**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2061){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} for emergencies, [**Prayer of Healing**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=596){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} for the group and [**Power Word: Shield**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} to absorb damage. In Classic it was the backbone of every raid, and it is likely to be again in Forever.

**A good fit if** you want to be the classic healer and like having the right spell for every situation.

## Paladin: efficient and unshakable

The [Paladin](/en/forever/classes/paladin) heals single targets, usually the tank, very mana efficiently with [**Flash of Light**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19750){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Holy Light**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=635){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. In emergencies [**Lay on Hands**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=633){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} saves instantly, and its blessings strengthen the whole group. It also wears plate and can take a lot.

**A good fit if** you want to focus on the tank. New in Forever: with the **Undead Paladin** it is available to the Horde for the first time.

## Shaman: healing for the whole group

The [Shaman](/en/forever/classes/shaman) shines with [**Chain Heal**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1064){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} when several players take damage, and [**Healing Wave**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=331){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} gives it a strong single-target heal. Its totems strengthen the group, and [**Mana Tide Totem**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16190){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} gives everyone mana back.

**A good fit if** you want to keep an eye on the whole group. New in Forever: the **Dwarf Shaman** brings the Shaman to the Alliance for the first time.

## Druid: healing over time

The [Druid](/en/forever/classes/druid) heals mostly with effects over time: [**Rejuvenation**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=774){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Regrowth**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8936){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} work ahead of the damage, [**Swiftmend**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18562){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} helps in emergencies. [**Innervate**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=29166){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} gives other healers mana, and [**Rebirth**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20484){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} brings the fallen back in the middle of a fight.

**A good fit if** you like to plan ahead and want to roam as a bear or cat now and then.

## Tips for new healers in WoW Forever

1. **Watch your mana.** Small, targeted heals get you further than big spells on full health.
2. **Drink between fights.** Let your group know when you need mana.
3. **Stand safe.** Stay in range of the tank, but away from the enemies.
4. **Heal ahead.** You can often anticipate damage on the tank before it hits.
