WoW Forever has **10 races** and **9 classes**, for a total of **56 playable combinations**. Compared with the original Classic, it adds six new combinations and the new Skyborne race. You can find the full checklist table on the [classes in WoW Forever](/en/forever/classes#voelker) page.

## The new combinations

| Race | New class | Faction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human | [Hunter](/en/forever/classes/hunter) | Alliance |
| Dwarf | [Shaman](/en/forever/classes/shaman) | Alliance |
| Gnome | [Priest](/en/forever/classes/priest) | Alliance |
| Orc | [Mage](/en/forever/classes/mage) | Horde |
| Undead | [Paladin](/en/forever/classes/paladin) | Horde |
| Troll | [Warlock](/en/forever/classes/warlock) | Horde |

The highlights: the **Paladin** is playable on both sides for the first time, and the Alliance gets its first Shaman with the **Dwarf Shaman**.

## The new race: Skyborne

The **Skyborne** are WoW Forever's new race. Depending on faction they join a different group and play five classes each:

- **Alliance (High Order):** Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Mage, Druid
- **Horde (Windshaper):** Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Druid
