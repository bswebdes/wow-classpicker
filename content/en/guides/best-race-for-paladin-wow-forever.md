The Paladin has the smallest choice of any class in WoW Forever: **only three races** can play it. In return there is a change people have talked about since BlizzCon: **Undead Paladins.** For the first time the Horde has Paladins in classic Azeroth, and of all people it is the Forsaken, who revered the Light in life and lost it in death.

This guide is based on Blizzard's development notes from 26 and 27 October 2026, the state shortly before launch on **4 November 2026**. We will check it against the live game after launch.

## The short answer

::race-picks{races="human,undead,dwarf" mode="forever" reasons="Alliance: best all-rounder for Holy, Protection and Retribution|Horde: the only choice, strong with Touch of the Grave|Alliance: Stoneform for tanks, Find Treasure while leveling"}
::

- **Alliance:** **Human** for all three specializations, **Dwarf** for Protection and dangerous poisons or bleeds.
- **Horde:** **Undead**, there is no other option.
- **Important:** the differences are small. RankedBoost puts Human and Undead level, with the Dwarf just behind.

## Who can play Paladin in WoW Forever?

| Race | Faction | New in Forever? | Start |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human | Alliance | no | Northshire, then the Cathedral of Light in Stormwind |
| Dwarf | Alliance | no | Coldridge Valley (Dun Morogh), then Ironforge |
| Undead | Horde | **yes** | Deathknell, then Brill and the Undercity |

Skyborne cannot be Paladins; the High Order plays Mage instead and the Windshapers Shaman. Every combination is listed in our guide to [races and classes in WoW Forever](/en/guides/wow-forever-races-classes).

## What changes for Paladins in Forever

A few new rules make the race choice a bit different from Classic:

- **Protection is a real tank.** [Seal of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311649) from level 10 gives you an absorb shield with every swing while you wear a shield. According to the development notes it also makes your [Judgement](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20271) taunt enemies at up to 10 yards. [Righteous Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=25780) raises the threat of your Holy attacks by 90 percent, and [Templar's Bulwark](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1311015) gives you a shield equal to your maximum health for 8 seconds.
- **Holy regenerates through Spirit.** With the [Reverence](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1310899) talent, 30 percent of your mana regeneration continues while casting. That suddenly makes extra Spirit valuable for healers.
- **Judgement no longer consumes your Seal.** You can judge more often without recasting the Seal.
- **No weapon racials.** The old Sword and Mace Specialization of Humans and Dwarves is gone.

## The Alliance races

### Human: the Paladin of the Silver Hand

Humans founded the **Knights of the Silver Hand**, and in the first year of WoW Forever the order is in hard times. Uther the Lightbringer refused to follow Arthas at Stratholme and later fell at Andorhal at the hand of his former student. His tomb lies in the Western Plaguelands. In Stormwind the Silver Hand gathers in the **Cathedral of Light**, where you train as a Human Paladin. At level 12, [The Tome of Divinity](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1642) sends you to Duthorian Rall, the classic start of your training.

In play the Human fits every specialization: [Ruthless](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20597) gives 1 percent critical strike chance for healing and damage (it was 2 percent during the beta). [The Human Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20598) increases your Spirit, which helps Holy Paladins most thanks to Reverence. In PvP, [Will to Survive](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259718) frees you from any stun, and [Perception](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20600) helps you spot stealthed rogues sooner. Warcraft Tavern and GamerBlurb recommend the Human as the best Alliance pick.

### Dwarf: the tank from Ironforge

The Dwarves of Ironforge have fought alongside the Silver Hand since the Second War. As a Dwarf you learn from Tiza Battleforge in the Hall of Mysteries, and your training also begins at level 12 with the [Tome of Divinity](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=2997).

The centerpiece is [Stoneform](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20594): it removes bleeds, poisons and diseases, briefly makes you immune to them and reduces physical damage taken by 10 percent for 8 seconds. For a Protection Paladin that is a strong defensive cooldown, and Warcraft Tavern highlights the new 10 percent for tanks in particular. Since 26 October the Dwarf also gets 1 percent hit chance (Artisan's Precision, no Wowhead page yet), so your taunt misses less often. [Big Game Hunter](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1259721) gives 5 percent more damage against beasts, and [Find Treasure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2481) shows you treasure and sometimes grants extra loot from chests.

## The Horde: the Undead Paladin

### Undead: the Light after death

The Forsaken were once citizens of Lordaeron, many of them devout servants of the Light. In WoW Forever some of them find their way back, and that is exactly what their class quest chain is about. You begin in the chapel of **Deathknell** with **Aramis Hammerhand**, who greets you with "Welcome back to the light, friend."

The quests revolve around a question Paladins never normally have to ask: what is the Light to someone who has already died?

- In [A Difficult Path](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98601) you read a consecrated scroll, and even a Forsaken shadow priest admits that Paladins make him uneasy.
- In [Rediscovering the Light](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=90902) you heal injured Deathguards with [Holy Light](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=635). The quest warns you that channeling the Light may cause you considerable pain now, and that you must push through it.
- [Coming to Terms](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=91208) and [A Light in the Darkness](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98389) show how hard it is for some to be "the very thing they spent their lives fighting against".
- [Continue Your Training](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=91209) takes you to Shari Stilwell in Brill, where you learn that "there are many in the Dark Lady's service who distrust us and our proximity to the light."
- At level 22, Morbin Lightbane in the Undercity sends you out in [Light's Justice](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92421). His creed: "As a paladin of the Forsaken, it is my duty to exact the will of our Dark Lady… For Lordaeron!"

From level 40 you summon your [Warhorse](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1279399), and at level 60 the Undead Paladin gets a mount of their own: the [Forsaken Charger](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1296534). Humans and Dwarves get the classic [Charger](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=23214) instead.

The racials fit the Paladin surprisingly well. [Touch of the Grave](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1260189) gives your spells and attacks a 20 percent chance to drain health from the target, up to 5 percent of your maximum health. That is the version Paladins share with Warriors and Rogues, and according to Warcraft Tavern it is especially strong for tanks. [Cannibalize](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20577) restores 7 percent of your health **and mana** every 2 seconds for 10 seconds, very handy while leveling. In PvP, [Will of the Forsaken](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=7744) frees you from fear, charm and sleep.

## The lore in year one

WoW Forever is set in the first year of World of Warcraft, and for Paladins it is a dark time:

- **Uther is dead**, and Arthas has become the Lich King himself in icy Northrend.
- **Alexandros Mograine**, the Ashbringer, was betrayed and killed by his own son Renault.
- **The Scarlet Crusade** under Saidan Dathrohan, in truth the dreadlord Balnazzar, fights fanatically against everything undead and holds parts of Tirisfal and the Plaguelands. Those who turned away from it founded the **Argent Dawn**.
- **Tirion Fordring** was cast out of the order for sparing the orc Eitrigg and lives in exile.

This is where the Undead Paladin becomes fascinating: they serve the Light, but side by side with the very people the Scarlet Crusade wants to destroy. Blizzard calls WoW Forever a **new branch** of World of Warcraft that does not exist in the same continuity as the current game. Undead Paladins in classic Azeroth are one of the things that only exist there.

## Our tip

If you simply want the best Paladin for healing, damage and tanking, pick the **Human** on the Alliance side. If you mainly want to tank, the **Dwarf** with Stoneform serves you very well. And if you want to play Horde or are looking for a story that has never been told this way, the **Undead Paladin** is the most exciting new combination in WoW Forever.
