## What is WoW Class Picker?

Can't decide which class to play in **World of Warcraft: Midnight**? Then let the classes settle it among themselves. In WoW Class Picker you choose your favorites, give them names and send them into a retro-style arena. Each class fights with four signature abilities, and the last one standing becomes your new main.

## How it works

1. **Pick classes:** click the classes you are considering, or hit "All in" to send all 13 classes into the fight.
2. **Name them:** give your fighters custom names or generate random ones.
3. **Start the battle:** follow the fight in the combat log and on the DPS and healing meter.
4. **Main found:** the last survivor is your class for Midnight.
