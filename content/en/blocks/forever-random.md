## Random race and class for WoW Forever

Want to try something new in Forever? The generator rolls a complete combination of race, class and talent tree, picked only from the 56 combinations that exist in WoW Forever. Use the filters to narrow it down by faction, role or armor.

For an overview of every combination, see our guide to [races and classes in WoW Forever](/en/guides/wow-forever-races-classes).
