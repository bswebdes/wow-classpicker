## How to read the overview

Every class in World of Warcraft has several **specializations** that decide which role you play in a group: tank, healer or damage. **Armor** determines your gear and says a lot about sturdiness: plate is the heaviest, cloth the lightest.

The **Beginner friendly** column is our take on how easy a class is to learn. More points mean friendlier for beginners. For a detailed recommendation, see our guide to the [easiest classes for beginners](/en/guides/easiest-wow-class-for-beginners).
