## What is Find Your Main?

Find Your Main is a WoW class picker and fan project for everyone who can't decide on a class in World of Warcraft. It started as a small arena battle where classes fight each other. It now also has a class quiz, a random class generator and guides for current WoW (Midnight) and WoW Forever.

## Who is behind it?

Find Your Main is a private, non-commercial fan project by Benny. The site is made for the love of the game and is free to use, with no sign-up. Questions, ideas or bugs? Write to [findyourmain@pm.me](mailto:findyourmain@pm.me).

## Where does the information come from?

- **Classes, specializations and races** come from the game itself and from Blizzard's official announcements. For WoW Forever, information reflects the beta.
- **Ratings** for beginner friendliness and solo play are our editorial assessment. They are meant to help you choose and are not a DPS ranking. See [how we rate classes](#how-we-rate-classes) below.
- **Images** of classes and races come from the official World of Warcraft website and are © Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. Blizzard allows their use on non-commercial fan sites.
- **Tuning numbers and tier lists** are deliberately left out, because they change with every patch.

## How we rate classes

Every class page shows two ratings from 1 to 5. They describe how a class plays, not how much damage it does right now.

**Beginner friendly** asks: how quickly will you feel at home with the class? We look at four things:

1. **Forgiveness:** How much can the class take, and how hard does it punish mistakes? A plate wearer with self-healing forgives more than a cloth wearer.
2. **Rotation:** How many abilities do you need to track, and how much do they depend on each other? A few clear priorities are easier than long sequences with cooldowns that have to line up.
3. **Resources and procs:** Do you need to plan a resource carefully or react to random effects? That takes attention early on.
4. **Positioning:** Do you have to stand behind the enemy, dodge in melee or stand still to cast? Ranged with freedom to move is the easiest.

A class has several specializations that differ in difficulty. The rating follows the specialization that is easiest to start with. Each class page describes how every specialization feels.

**Solo play** asks: how well do you get through quests, world content and Delves on your own? Self-healing, a companion that soaks damage, sturdiness and ways to escape all count.

**Where the values come from:** We set the first values from our own assessment. In October 2026 we compared them with common community assessments for the current patch, for example [Skill-Capped's difficulty tier list](https://www.skill-capped.com/wowarticles/tier-lists/difficulty/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and beginner and solo guides from large WoW sites. There, Beast Mastery Hunter, Fury Warrior and Protection Paladin are seen as especially beginner friendly, while Feral Druid, Arcane Mage, Outlaw Rogue and Enhancement Shaman are seen as especially demanding. Blood Death Knight, Hunter, Vengeance Demon Hunter, Guardian Druid and Paladin are considered strong solo. Our values match this. For WoW Forever, Classic experience applies: Hunter and Warlock level solo most comfortably, while the Warrior needs patience and good gear.

The ratings remain subjective. If a class is fun for you, it is the right one, no matter what a table says.
