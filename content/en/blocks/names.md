## How to find a good WoW name

A good name fits your race and class, is easy to read in chat and sounds like it belongs in Azeroth. Pick a race and a style above: **epic** for heroes, **dark** for warlocks and death knights, **lore** for names that could come from an NPC, and **funny** for your next goblin.

Short and common fantasy names have been taken on most realms for years. That is why the generator prefers longer, unusual names, and why every name has variants with accents or a different spelling.
