title: "Random class"
faq:
  - q: How random is the random class generator?
    a: Every class and spec combination that matches your filters has the same chance. Classes with more matching specs show up a little more often, like the Druid with four specs.
  - q: What is a random class good for?
    a: Your next alt, challenges with friends or on stream, or when you simply want to play something you would never try otherwise.
  - q: Can I roll only tanks or healers?
    a: Yes. Choose Tank, Healer or DPS as the role and the generator only rolls matching specs. You can also filter by armor type.
---

## The random WoW class generator

Sometimes the best decision is not to decide yourself. The generator rolls a random class and spec from all 40 specs in Midnight. Use the filters to narrow it down to a role or armor type, perfect when your group still needs a healer.

Like the result? The [class page](/en/classes) tells you how the class plays. And if you like two rolls, let them fight in the [arena](/en).
