## How It Feels

Survival is the Hunter who prefers to get hands-on. Instead of shooting from afar, you leap to the enemy with Harpoon, stab with your spear and throw bombs that set packs on fire. Your pet fights shoulder to shoulder with you.

That gives the spec a feel of its own: less shooting range, more fight for survival in the wild. Survival is a bit unusual and less common than the other Hunters, but it has a loyal fan base.

## How Survival Works

Survival uses **Focus** but fights in melee:

1. **Close in:** Harpoon pulls you to the enemy and roots them briefly.
2. **Build Focus:** Kill Command sends your pet at the target and generates Focus.
3. **Spend Focus:** Raptor Strike is your main melee attack.
4. **Bombs:** Wildfire Bomb explodes in a cone and burns enemies over time. It deals extra damage to the main target.
5. **Burst window:** Coordinated Assault has you and your pet attack together and strengthens your bond, so both deal more damage afterward.

## Key Abilities

- [**Raptor Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186270){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your main attack, a vicious slash with your weapon.
- [**Wildfire Bomb**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259495){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a bomb that explodes in a cone and sets enemies on fire.
- [**Kill Command**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259489){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your pet attacks and generates Focus.

- [**Coordinated Assault**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=360952){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: you and your pet charge together and deal more damage afterward.
- [**Freezing Trap**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187650){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a trap that incapacitates the first enemy to step on it.

## Lore: Azeroth's Survivalists

Survival draws on the Hunters who live not in castles but in the wild: trappers, trackers and warriors who make do with what the land gives them. Among the **Zandalari** and the jungle trolls of Stranglethorn, the spear has always been the weapon of the hunt, among the Tauren the polearm.

In Legion, Survival Hunters fought with **Talonclaw**, the legendary Spear of the Wild Gods. That closeness to the wild gods, the ancients of Azeroth, still suits the spec today.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- a unique melee fantasy with a pet at your side,
- strong against groups with bombs,
- good control with traps and Harpoon,
- mail armor and Aspect of the Turtle as an emergency brake.

**Weaknesses**

- more complex than Beast Mastery,
- less common, so fewer guides and role models,
- in melee you are closer to danger than the other Hunters.

## Who Is the Survival Hunter For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want a melee spec with its own personality,
- like fighting together with a pet,
- love bombs, traps and a wilderness feel.

**Probably not if you …**

- want a classic ranged Hunter, in which case [Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) or [Marksmanship](/en/classes/hunter/marksmanship) fits,
- want an especially easy spec.

## Survival, Beast Mastery or Marksmanship?

[Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) and [Marksmanship](/en/classes/hunter/marksmanship) fight from range. Survival is the choice for everyone who wants to bring the Hunter fantasy into melee.
