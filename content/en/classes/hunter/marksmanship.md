## How It Feels

Marksmanship is the sharpshooter who fires the one decisive shot from far away. With bow, crossbow or gun you take aim, wait a moment and then release a hit that knocks enemies off their feet. In between, quick volleys pelt the target.

It feels calmer and more deliberate than Beast Mastery. Some of your shots need a short cast time, so positioning matters. If you like waiting for the perfect moment for the big hit, this is your spec.

## How Marksmanship Works

Marksmanship also uses **Focus**:

1. **Aimed Shot:** your heaviest shot, with a cast time and charges. It is the heart of the spec.
2. **Rapid Fire:** a quick stream of shots that works on the move and generates Focus.
3. **Spend Focus:** Arcane Shot is your quick attack for spare Focus and can reduce the cooldown of Rapid Fire.
4. **Trueshot:** your big cooldown, more critical strikes and much faster recharging Aimed Shots and Rapid Fire.

Against groups, Volley rains arrows on an area and makes your next shots spread to several targets.

## Key Abilities

- [**Aimed Shot**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=19434){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a powerful aimed shot with a short cast time.
- [**Rapid Fire**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=257044){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a stream of shots, usable on the move.
- [**Arcane Shot**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185358){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a quick shot dealing Arcane damage.
- [**Trueshot**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=288613){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: more critical strike chance and much faster cooldowns for your key shots.
- [**Volley**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=260243){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a hail of arrows that hits all enemies in the area.
- [**Aspect of the Turtle**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186265){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: makes every attack miss you, your emergency brake.

## Lore: The Rangers of Quel'Thalas

Hardly any people stand for the bow like the elves of Quel'Thalas. The **Farstriders** under Ranger-General **Sylvanas Windrunner** defended the elven kingdom until Arthas ravaged it in the Third War. Sylvanas later became the Banshee Queen and leader of the Forsaken, but her reputation as Azeroth's greatest archer remained.

That makes WoW Midnight especially exciting, because the expansion returns to Quel'Thalas, home of the rangers. The Dwarves with their Ironforge guns and the Night Elf Sentinels carry the tradition of ranged hunters on as well.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- long range, you hit enemies before they reach you,
- satisfying, heavy hits,
- clear burst windows with Trueshot,
- Rapid Fire and Arcane Shot work on the move.

**Weaknesses**

- Aimed Shot requires standing still,
- less protection from a pet while solo than Beast Mastery,
- positioning matters more for your damage.

## Who Is the Marksmanship Hunter For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want to play the sharpshooter,
- love big, planned hits,
- are a fan of Sylvanas and the rangers.

**Probably not if you …**

- want to be on the move constantly, in which case [Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) fits better,
- prefer fighting alongside a strong companion.

## Marksmanship, Beast Mastery or Survival?

[Beast Mastery](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) is the Hunter with a pack and full freedom of movement, [Survival](/en/classes/hunter/survival) the melee with spear and bombs. Marksmanship is the choice for the classic sharpshooter.
