## How It Feels

Elemental is the Shaman who unleashes the forces of nature. Lightning jumps from enemy to enemy, lava crashes down in critical strikes, and the earth breaks open beneath your foes. You fight from range and call on powers far older than you.

It feels powerful and easy to understand. Your key spells each have a clear job, and when a Lava Burst crits, it really lands.

## How Elemental Works

The Elemental Shaman uses **Maelstrom**:

1. **Flame Shock:** burns the target over time and makes Lava Burst always crit.
2. **Lava Burst:** your strongest spell against targets with Flame Shock.
3. **Build Maelstrom:** Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning generate Maelstrom.
4. **Spend Maelstrom:** Earth Shock against one target, Earthquake against groups.
5. **Big moments:** Stormkeeper makes your next lightning spells instant and much stronger, the Fire Elemental fights at your side for a while.

## Key Abilities

- [**Flame Shock**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188389){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: burns the target over time and sets up guaranteed critical Lava Bursts.
- [**Lava Burst**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51505){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: molten lava that always crits against targets with Flame Shock.
- [**Lightning Bolt**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188196){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your basic spell, a bolt of lightning.
- [**Chain Lightning**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188443){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: lightning that jumps to more enemies.
- [**Earth Shock**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8042){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: an instant shock that spends Maelstrom.
- [**Earthquake**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=61882){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: makes the earth tremble beneath groups of enemies.
- [**Stormkeeper**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191634){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your next lightning spells are instant and deal much more damage.

## Lore: The Earthen Ring

Shamans speak with the elements: earth, fire, water and air. Their most famous representative is **Thrall**, who refounded the Horde and later tried to heal Azeroth after the Cataclysm with the **Earthen Ring**. Elemental is the side of the Shaman that wields these powers directly as a weapon.

In Legion, Elemental Shamans carried **The Fist of Ra-den**, the weapon of a titan keeper who commanded lightning and storm. Shamans exist among Orcs, Tauren, Trolls, Dwarves, Draenei and many other races, each culture with its own view of the elements.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- a clear, easy-to-understand loop,
- strong against groups thanks to Chain Lightning and Earthquake,
- mail armor and heals for emergencies,
- satisfying, guaranteed critical Lava Bursts.

**Weaknesses**

- many spells require standing still,
- less mobile than a Hunter or Devourer,
- you have to keep Flame Shock up constantly.

## Who Is the Elemental Shaman For?

**A good fit if you …**

- love lightning, lava and the forces of nature,
- want an understandable ranged spec,
- enjoy the fantasy of the Shaman and the elements.

**Probably not if you …**

- want to be on the move a lot, in which case the [Beast Mastery Hunter](/en/classes/hunter/beast-mastery) fits,
- prefer melee, in which case [Enhancement](/en/classes/shaman/enhancement) is the pick.

## Elemental, Enhancement or Restoration?

[Enhancement](/en/classes/shaman/enhancement) is the dual-wielding melee, [Restoration](/en/classes/shaman/restoration) the healer with totems. Elemental is the choice for everyone who wants to unleash the elements from range.
