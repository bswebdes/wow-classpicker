## How It Feels

The Restoration Shaman heals with the power of water. Healing waves roll over your allies, rain falls on the group, and totems stand like beacons on the battlefield. You do not just heal individual players, you shape the space the group fights in.

It feels powerful and group-focused. You are especially strong when the group stands close together, because then your heals and totems jump from one player to the next.

## How Restoration Works

The Shaman heals with **mana** and totems:

1. **Riptide:** an instant heal with extra healing over time, ideal for setting up.
2. **Chain Heal:** heals your target and jumps to the three most injured allies nearby.
3. **Healing Rain:** heals up to six allies in an area over time.
4. **Healing Wave:** an efficient single-target heal.
5. **Totems:** Healing Stream Totem heals continuously, Healing Tide Totem heals the whole group strongly, Spirit Link Totem evens out health.

## Lore: The Spirit of Water

For Shamans, water stands for life and healing. Restoration Shamans ask the spirits of water for help so their allies can keep fighting. That tradition is found among the Tauren of Mulgore as well as the Draenei, whose prophet **Nobundo** was the first of his people to find the path of the elements in Outland.

In Legion, Restoration Shamans carried **Sharas'dal, Scepter of Tides**, a weapon from the depths of the sea. **Thrall** and the **Earthen Ring** stand for this healing side as well: after the Cataclysm, the Shamans tried to heal the wounded world itself.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- strong group healing, especially when stacked,
- many totems with unique effects,
- mail, sturdier than cloth wearers,
- Spirit Link Totem as a special rescue tool.

**Weaknesses**

- totems are tied to one spot,
- weaker when the group is spread out,
- slow solo.

## Who Is the Restoration Shaman For?

**A good fit if you …**

- like healing groups rather than single targets,
- love totems and elemental spirits,
- enjoy keeping an eye on your group's positioning.

**Probably not if you …**

- prefer healing proactively over time, in which case the [Restoration Druid](/en/classes/druid/restoration) fits,
- want a very mobile healer, such as the [Mistweaver](/en/classes/monk/mistweaver).

## Restoration, Elemental or Enhancement?

[Elemental](/en/classes/shaman/elemental) fights with lightning from range, [Enhancement](/en/classes/shaman/enhancement) in melee with two weapons. Restoration is the choice for everyone who wants to heal with the spirits of water.
