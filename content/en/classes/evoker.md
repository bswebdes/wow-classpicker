## Playstyle

The Evoker is WoW's newest class and closely tied to the draconic Dracthyr. It wields the magic of the dragonflights: fire, life, time and dreams. In mail armor it fights at **mid-range**, about 25 yards, closer than other casters.

Its standout feature is **empowered spells**: you hold the key, charge the spell through several stages and release it at the right moment. Besides Mana, the Evoker uses **Essence** as a resource. With **Soar** and **Hover** it is also very mobile.

## Specializations

- **Devastation** is the damage spec with Fire Breath and Disintegrate. Strong against groups of enemies and full of burst.
- **Preservation** heals with dream and time magic. With empowered heals and time spells, the Evoker can even rewind damage.
- **Augmentation** is unique: a damage spec that mainly makes other players stronger. Its contribution often doesn't show up properly on damage meters.

## Who is the Evoker for?

**A good fit if you …**

- are fascinated by dragons and time magic,
- want a fresh casting style with charge-up spells,
- want to choose between damage, healing and support.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank, since the Evoker has no tank spec,
- are used to the full range of classic casters and don't want to get closer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Evoker plays unlike any other class, and Augmentation offers a role that doesn't exist anywhere else. Its shorter range demands good positioning, though, and empowered spells take some getting used to.
