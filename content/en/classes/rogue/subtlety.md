## How It Feels

Subtlety is the ninja. You vanish into the shadows mid-fight, reappear behind your enemy and strike before they understand what happened. Shadow clones fight at your side, and for a brief moment you feel unstoppable.

It is all about **windows**. Between the big moments you set everything up, and when the window opens, your strongest attacks follow in a few seconds. When such a window lands perfectly, it is one of the most satisfying feelings in WoW.

## How Subtlety Works

Subtlety uses **energy** and **combo points** and builds around shadow windows:

1. **Prepare:** outside of windows you build combo points with Backstab and keep energy ready.
2. **Shadow Dance:** for a few seconds you can use Stealth abilities in the middle of combat.
3. **Shadowstrike:** from the shadows you teleport behind your target and deal extra damage.
4. **Finishers:** Eviscerate spends combo points, Secret Technique summons shadow clones that strike with you.
5. **Empower:** Symbols of Death and Shadow Blades make your window even stronger.

## Key Abilities

- [**Shadow Dance**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185313){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: lets you use Stealth abilities in the middle of combat.
- [**Shadowstrike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185438){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: strikes from the shadows behind your target and generates two combo points.
- [**Backstab**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=53){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your basic attack, stronger when you stand behind the enemy.
- [**Eviscerate**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196819){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your main finisher, stronger with every combo point.
- [**Symbols of Death**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=212283){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: restores energy and empowers your finishers.
- [**Shadow Blades**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121471){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your weapons deal extra Shadow damage and you generate double combo points.
- [**Secret Technique**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=280719){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a finisher in which shadow clones attack alongside you.

## Lore: Spies and Shadows

Subtlety stands for the Rogues who work in secret: spies, saboteurs and shadow warriors. For the Alliance that is the intelligence service **SI:7** under spymaster **Mathias Shaw**, for the Horde the scouts and agents who work in the dark for their leaders.

A well-known shadow fighter is **Valeera Sanguinar**, a Blood Elf who went down in history as a gladiator at the side of Varian Wrynn. In WoW Midnight, set in her homeland of Quel'Thalas, the spec of Blood Elf Rogues is more topical than ever.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- explosive burst windows, very satisfying with good timing,
- a strong ninja fantasy with shadow clones,
- Stealth and lots of control over enemies,
- traditionally very dangerous in PvP.

**Weaknesses**

- demanding, bad timing costs a lot of damage,
- little damage outside of windows,
- leather, less sturdy than plate wearers.

## Who Is the Subtlety Rogue For?

**A good fit if you …**

- love timing and perfectly prepared moments,
- enjoy the ninja and spy fantasy,
- are interested in PvP.

**Probably not if you …**

- prefer even damage without windows, in which case [Outlaw](/en/classes/rogue/outlaw) fits,
- want a beginner friendly spec.

## Subtlety, Assassination or Outlaw?

[Assassination](/en/classes/rogue/assassination) is the calm assassin with poisons, [Outlaw](/en/classes/rogue/outlaw) the pirate with dice and pistols. Subtlety is the choice for everyone who wants to strike from the shadows.
