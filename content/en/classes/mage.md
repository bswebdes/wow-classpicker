## Playstyle

The Mage is the classic spellcaster: robe, staff and an arsenal of arcane, fire and frost spells. It fights from range and deals enormous damage in short burst windows. Its main resource is **Mana**.

Mages are loved for their comfort tools: **Portals** take them and others to the capital cities, they conjure food and drink, and **Arcane Intellect** buffs the whole group. In combat, **Blink** and **Ice Block** get them out of trouble, and **Polymorph** takes an enemy out of the fight for a while.

## Specializations

- **Arcane** juggles Mana and builds toward big burst phases. If you like resource management, this is your spec.
- **Fire** thrives on critical strikes and chain reactions. When everything lines up, the screen explodes in flames.
- **Frost** slows and freezes enemies, then shatters them. Frost is considered the most controlled and often the most approachable Mage spec.

## Who is the Mage for?

**A good fit if you …**

- want to cast spells from range,
- love burst damage and big numbers,
- value comfort like portals and conjured food.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank or heal,
- want a sturdy class, since the Mage cannot take much in cloth.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Mage is strong in groups and in PvP and offers three very different casting styles. Solo, it needs to rely on control and positioning, as it has very little self-healing.
