## How It Feels

Feral is the Druid as a predator. In Cat Form you stalk your prey, pounce and tear wounds that bleed for a long time. You are fast, silent and dangerous, and when everything comes together, your enemy is down before they can really react.

It feels demanding and rewarding. You manage several bleeds at once, watch energy and combo points and plan when to apply your wounds at their strongest.

## How Feral Works

The Feral Druid uses **energy** and **combo points**:

1. **Rake:** a bleed that awards a combo point.
2. **Build combo points:** Shred is your basic attack and grants more combo points.
3. **Finishers:** Rip applies a long bleed, Ferocious Bite deals instant damage and can consume extra energy.
4. **Tiger's Fury:** restores energy and increases your damage, including for bleeds you apply during it.
5. **Berserk:** your big cooldown, generating combo points at high speed.

## Lore: Ashamane, the Cat of the Wild

Feral is the path of the predator. Druids who choose it honor the wild gods of the hunt. At its center is **Ashamane**, one of Azeroth's wild gods, revered as a great cat.

In Legion, Feral Druids carried the **Fangs of Ashamane** as their artifact, claws that hold the goddess's power. Druids once learned Cat Form from the demigods of nature, who entrusted their secrets to Malfurion and his students.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- very rewarding when the bleeds land well,
- stealth in Cat Form,
- Druid tools like healing and Bear Form for emergencies,
- fast travel in cat and travel forms.

**Weaknesses**

- one of the most demanding specs,
- keeping track of several effects at once takes practice,
- in Cat Form you see none of your gear.

## Who Is the Feral Druid For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want a demanding melee with lots of depth,
- love predators and cats,
- enjoy bleeds and effects over time.

**Probably not if you …**

- want an easy start, in which case [Fury](/en/classes/warrior/fury) or [Havoc](/en/classes/demon-hunter/havoc) fits,
- want to see your armor in combat.

## Feral, Balance, Guardian or Restoration?

[Balance](/en/classes/druid/balance) fights from range as a Moonkin, [Guardian](/en/classes/druid/guardian) tanks as a bear, [Restoration](/en/classes/druid/restoration) heals. Feral is the choice for everyone who wants to hunt as a cat.
