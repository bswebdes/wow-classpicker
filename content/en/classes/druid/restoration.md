## How It Feels

The Restoration Druid heals like nature itself: patient, steady and forward-looking. You place Rejuvenations on your allies, let flowers bloom on the tank and let your healing carry the group before the big damage even arrives.

It feels proactive. Instead of reacting to falling health bars, you are already there when it happens. That takes some practice and overview, but it is especially fun if you like being one step ahead.

## How Restoration Works

The Druid heals with **mana** and effects over time:

1. **Rejuvenation:** heals over time, you spread it across many targets.
2. **Lifebloom:** heals over time and heals strongly once more at the end. You usually keep it on the tank.
3. **Wild Growth:** heals up to five allies over time, with the healing strongest at the start.
4. **Instant healing:** Swiftmend consumes one of your effects over time for an instant strong heal, Regrowth heals directly and over time.
5. **Emergencies:** Tranquility heals all nearby allies, Ironbark protects a target.

## Lore: G'Hanir and the Emerald Dream

Restoration is the heart of druidism. Druids guard the balance of nature, and their healing power comes from the **Emerald Dream**, the primordial version of Azeroth guarded by the green dragonflight.

In Legion, Restoration Druids carried **G'Hanir, the Mother Tree** as their artifact, a staff holding the life force of an ancient tree. The first mortal Druid, **Malfurion Stormrage**, learned to heal nature under the demigod **Cenarius**, and Restoration Druids carry that tradition on to this day.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- strong, steady healing over time,
- many heals possible on the move,
- Druid tools like Bear Form for emergencies,
- the iconic tree form.

**Weaknesses**

- proactive healing takes practice,
- sudden, big damage is harder to catch,
- slow solo.

## Who Is the Restoration Druid For?

**A good fit if you …**

- like planning ahead and being one step ahead,
- love nature and healing over time,
- want to heal a lot on the move.

**Probably not if you …**

- prefer reactive play with direct heals, in which case the [Holy Paladin](/en/classes/paladin/holy) or [Holy Priest](/en/classes/priest/holy) fits,
- like a healer with lots of totems, such as the [Restoration Shaman](/en/classes/shaman/restoration).

## Restoration, Balance, Feral or Guardian?

With four specs, the Druid can play every role. [Balance](/en/classes/druid/balance) fights from range, [Feral](/en/classes/druid/feral) as a cat, [Guardian](/en/classes/druid/guardian) tanks as a bear.
