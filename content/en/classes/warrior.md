## Playstyle

The Warrior is melee in its purest form: heavy plate, big weapons and no spellbook. Its resource is **Rage**, which builds as you deal and take damage. The longer a fight goes, the more of your toolkit opens up.

**Charge** and **Heroic Leap** get you into the fight fast, and abilities like **Victory Rush** heal you along the way. That makes solo play much smoother than you would expect from a class without healing spells.

## Specializations

- **Arms** swings one big two-handed weapon and lands heavy single hits. It plays in a planned, rhythmic way: apply your debuffs, then hit as hard as you can.
- **Fury** wields two weapons and plays fast and frantic. While enraged, the Warrior hits nonstop and heals in the process.
- **Protection** is the shield-and-board tank spec. Protection Warriors block, absorb and hold packs of enemies in place with taunts and area damage.

## Who is the Warrior for?

**A good fit if you …**

- want to jump straight into melee without juggling spell bars,
- are looking for a sturdy class to learn the game with,
- want to tank later but start out dealing damage.

**Not ideal if you …**

- prefer fighting from a safe distance,
- want to heal, because Warriors have no healing spec.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Its biggest strength is a simple core: the basic rotation clicks quickly, and plate armor forgives mistakes. **Battle Shout** is a group buff every party is happy to have. Things get harder when enemies stay at range, since the Warrior has very few ranged options.
