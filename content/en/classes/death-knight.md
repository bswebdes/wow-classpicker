## Playstyle

::class-art{name="deathknight"}
::

The Death Knight is a fallen hero who returned as one of the undead. It fights in plate with runeblades and dark magic of frost, blood and plague. Its resources are **Runes**, which recharge on their own, and **Runic Power**, which you earn by spending runes.

Signature tools are [**Death Grip**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49576){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which pulls enemies to you, and [**Death Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which heals more the more damage you have recently taken. [**Anti-Magic Shell**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48707){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} protects against spells. Death Knights start their journey in their own starting zone with a strong story.

## Background and lore

Death knights are fallen heroes whom the **Lich King** raised as undead warriors. Their stronghold was the necropolis **Acherus**. Under **Darion Mograine** they broke free of him at Light's Hope Chapel and founded the **Knights of the Ebon Blade**. Since then they have fought of their own will. Death knights use **Runes** and **Runic Power** and are the only class with Blood, Frost and Unholy magic.

## Specializations

### Blood

Blood is the vampire among tanks: the more damage you take, the more you heal. You build bone shields, drain life from enemies and in an emergency let a rune weapon dance for you. Nearly unkillable solo, which is why it tops almost every solo guide.

**Signature abilities:** [**Marrowrend**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Heart Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Dancing Rune Weapon**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Frost

Frost wields two weapons or a big two-hander and strikes with icy blows. The playstyle is fast and direct. The fantasy: the cold, merciless knight from Northrend.

**Signature abilities:** [**Obliterate**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Pillar of Frost**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Unholy

Unholy is the necromancer in plate armor. A ghoul is always at your side, you spread plagues and summon the undead at the right moment. More to manage than Frost, but a very strong pet fantasy.

**Signature abilities:** [**Festering Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85948){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Apocalypse**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=275699){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Dark Transformation**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=63560){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

## Who is the Death Knight for?

**A good fit if you …**

- want a sturdy class that is very hard to kill in solo content,
- want to learn tanking, since Blood forgives a lot of mistakes,
- love the dark fantasy around the Lich King.

**Not ideal if you …**

- need high mobility, because the Death Knight is on the slower side,
- want to play at range or as a healer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Death Knight is tough, self-sufficient and can pull enemies out of position with Death Grip. Its biggest weakness is mobility: fast classes that stay at range give it a hard time.
