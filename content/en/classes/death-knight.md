## Playstyle

The Death Knight is a fallen hero who returned as one of the undead. It fights in plate with runeblades and dark magic of frost, blood and plague. Its resources are **Runes**, which recharge on their own, and **Runic Power**, which you earn by spending runes.

Signature tools are [**Death Grip**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49576){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which pulls enemies to you, and [**Death Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which heals more the more damage you have recently taken. [**Anti-Magic Shell**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48707){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} protects against spells. Death Knights start their journey in their own starting zone with a strong story.

## Specializations

- **Blood** is one of the most popular tank specs: huge self-healing, lots of control and a very sturdy feel.
- **Frost** wields two weapons or one big two-hander and strikes with icy blows. It plays fast and direct.
- **Unholy** commands undead minions, spreads plague and raises an entire army at the right moment.

## Who is the Death Knight for?

**A good fit if you …**

- want a sturdy class that is very hard to kill in solo content,
- want to learn tanking, since Blood forgives a lot of mistakes,
- love the dark fantasy around the Lich King.

**Not ideal if you …**

- need high mobility, because the Death Knight is on the slower side,
- want to play at range or as a healer.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Death Knight is tough, self-sufficient and can pull enemies out of position with Death Grip. Its biggest weakness is mobility: fast classes that stay at range give it a hard time.
