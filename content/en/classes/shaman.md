## Playstyle

The Shaman speaks with the elements: earth, fire, water and air fight at its side. In mail armor it stands between caster and fighter, and with three completely different specs it is one of the most versatile classes in the game.

Its trademark is **totems**, placed on the ground to empower allies or hinder enemies. Then there is **Bloodlust** or **Heroism**, a powerful group buff for the decisive moment. And if everything goes wrong, the Shaman can bring itself back with **Reincarnation**.

## Specializations

- **Elemental** hurls lightning and lava from range. Chain Lightning and Earthquake make it strong against groups of enemies.
- **Enhancement** is the melee Shaman with two weapons, channeling elemental magic into its strikes and unleashing it as instant spells.
- **Restoration** heals with waves, rain and totems. It shines when the group stands close together.

## Who is the Shaman for?

**A good fit if you …**

- want to choose between ranged, melee and healing,
- like supporting your group with buffs and utility,
- love the nature-bound fantasy of the elements.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank, since the Shaman has no tank spec,
- want a very simple rotation, as totems and priorities take time to learn.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Shaman brings a lot that groups love: Bloodlust, interrupts and useful totems. Thanks to self-healing and Reincarnation, it also handles solo play well. Mastering it takes some patience, and the reward is one of the most flexible classes in the game.
