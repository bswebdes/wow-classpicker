## Playstyle

The Rogue is the master of the shadows. It sneaks up in **Stealth**, opens with a surprise attack and is gone before the enemy can react. Its resources are **Energy** and **Combo Points**: quick attacks build points, and strong finishers spend them.

On top of that comes a huge toolbox of crowd control: **Sap**, **Blind**, **Kick** to interrupt and **Vanish** as an escape button. In dungeons, **Shroud of Concealment** can even stealth the whole group for a short time.

## Specializations

- **Assassination** coats its blades in poison and kills slowly but surely with bleeds and poisons.
- **Outlaw** is the pirate of the Rogues: sabers, pistol shots and a gamble with **Roll the Bones**.
- **Subtlety** keeps slipping back into the shadows and deals enormous burst damage in short windows.

## Who is the Rogue for?

**A good fit if you …**

- like controlling the fight and outplaying your enemies,
- enjoy PvP, where stealth and crowd control are worth their weight in gold,
- have fun managing resources in your rotation.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank or heal,
- want a sturdy class that forgives mistakes, since the Rogue is fragile in leather armor.

## Strengths and weaknesses

The Rogue rewards experience like few other classes: if you know your tools, you can win fights that look lost. For beginners, the learning curve is steeper than for a Warrior or Hunter, and without self-healing, solo play can get tight.
