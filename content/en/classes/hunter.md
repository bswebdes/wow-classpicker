## Playstyle

::class-art{name="hunter"}
::

The Hunter fights with a bow, gun or spear and often has an animal companion at its side. Its resource is **Focus**, which refills on its own over time. Because it attacks from range, it rarely stands where the damage is.

With [**Feign Death**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5384){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Disengage**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=781){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Aspect of the Turtle**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186265){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, the Hunter has strong tools to escape trouble. That makes it one of the safest classes for leveling and solo content.

## Background and lore

Hunters are trackers, marksmen and friends of beasts. The best known come from House **Windrunner**: **Sylvanas** was Ranger-General of Quel'Thalas before she became the Banshee Queen, and her sister **Alleria** is one of the Alliance's finest archers. On the Horde side, the half-ogre **Rexxar** and his bear Misha are the very picture of a beastmaster. Almost every race can be a hunter, and you tame companions all over the world.

## Specializations

### Beast Mastery

Beast Mastery is the pet specialization above all. Your pet deals a big part of your damage, and you can use almost every ability on the move. The fantasy is clear: you and your pack against everyone else. Almost every community list names Beast Mastery as the top pick for beginners.

**Signature abilities:** [**Bestial Wrath**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=19574){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Barbed Shot**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=217200){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Marksmanship

Marksmanship is the sharpshooter with bow or gun who fires heavy shots from long range. Some shots take a moment, so positioning matters. If you like waiting for the perfect moment for a big hit, this is your spec.

**Signature abilities:** [**Rapid Fire**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=257044){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Trueshot**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=288613){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Survival

Survival is the exception among hunters: a melee fighter with spear, traps and bombs who fights side by side with your pet. Wilderness instead of shooting range. A little unusual, but with a personality of its own.

**Signature abilities:** [**Raptor Strike**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186270){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Wildfire Bomb**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259495){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Harpoon**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190925){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

## Who is the Hunter for?

**A good fit if you …**

- are new to WoW and want a class that works right away,
- enjoy having a pet and collecting animals,
- play a lot of solo content, since your pet can hold enemies.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank or heal, because the Hunter is a pure damage class,
- are looking for a demanding rotation.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Few classes are as accessible as the Hunter, and taming pets is a collection game of its own. The flip side: if you want to try all three roles, you will need a second class eventually.
