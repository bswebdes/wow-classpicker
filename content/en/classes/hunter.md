## Playstyle

The Hunter fights with a bow, gun or spear and often has an animal companion at its side. Its resource is **Focus**, which refills on its own over time. Because it attacks from range, it rarely stands where the damage is.

With **Feign Death**, **Disengage** and **Aspect of the Turtle**, the Hunter has strong tools to escape trouble. That makes it one of the safest classes for leveling and solo content.

## Specializations

- **Beast Mastery** is the pet spec. Your animals deal a big share of your damage, and almost every ability can be used on the move. It is widely seen as one of the easiest specs in the game.
- **Marksmanship** focuses on precise, heavy shots and long range. Positioning matters, because some shots need a moment to fire.
- **Survival** is the odd one out: a melee fighter with a spear and a companion, using traps and bombs.

## Who is the Hunter for?

**A good fit if you …**

- are new to WoW and want a class that works right away,
- enjoy having a pet and collecting animals,
- play a lot of solo content, since your pet can hold enemies.

**Not ideal if you …**

- want to tank or heal, because the Hunter is a pure damage class,
- are looking for a demanding rotation.

## Strengths and weaknesses

Few classes are as accessible as the Hunter, and taming pets is a collection game of its own. The flip side: if you want to try all three roles, you will need a second class eventually.
