## How It Feels

Preservation heals with the power of life and time. Green dream magic flows over your allies in waves, bronze time magic doubles heals or turns back the last few seconds. This is not ordinary healing, but a game with time and life.

It feels creative and experimental. Empowered heals, Echoes and time spells give you lots of ways to solve a situation.

## How Preservation Works

The Preservation Evoker heals with **mana** and **Essence**:

1. **Echo:** heals a target and makes your next heal on it work a second time.
2. **Dream Breath:** an empowered healing breath that heals you and up to five allies in a cone in front of you.
3. **Spiritbloom:** an empowered heal that jumps to more allies at higher ranks.
4. **Reversion:** heals over time and makes up for part of recently taken damage.
5. **Time magic:** Time Dilation spreads damage on a target over time, Rewind partially undoes damage taken by the whole group.

## Lore: The Green and Bronze Flights

Preservation combines the powers of two dragonflights. The **green dragonflight** watches over the **Emerald Dream**, the primordial version of Azeroth, and stands for life and healing. The **bronze dragonflight** under **Nozdormu** guards the timeways and makes sure history runs its course.

The Dracthyr carry the essence of both flights, and Preservation Evokers use it to keep their allies alive. The ability to turn back damage is a direct legacy of the bronze dragons.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- creative healing with lots of options,
- strong emergency tools such as Rewind,
- Hover allows casting on the move,
- mail, sturdier than cloth wearers.

**Weaknesses**

- short range of 25 yards,
- empowered spells and Echo take practice,
- only Dracthyr can play Evokers.

## Who Is the Preservation Evoker For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want an unusual, creative healer,
- love dragons and time magic,
- like experimenting with tools.

**Probably not if you …**

- want an easy-to-understand first healer, in which case the [Holy Priest](/en/classes/priest/holy) fits,
- want to heal from long range.

## Preservation, Devastation or Augmentation?

[Devastation](/en/classes/evoker/devastation) deals damage with fire and arcane magic, [Augmentation](/en/classes/evoker/augmentation) empowers other players. Preservation is the choice for everyone who wants to heal with life and time.
