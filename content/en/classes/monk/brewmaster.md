## How It Feels

The Brewmaster is the tank who staggers into battle with a keg on its shoulder. It dodges, stumbles, smashes with kegs and breathes fire while gulping down its brews. It looks chaotic but is full of technique: hardly any tank handles incoming damage this actively.

What sets it apart is **Stagger**. Instead of simply taking or blocking damage, you spread it over time and decide when to wash it away with a brew. That is a game within the game and makes the Brewmaster the most exciting tank of all for many players.

## How Brewmaster Works

The Brewmaster uses **energy** and lots of cooldowns:

1. **Stagger:** part of the physical damage is delayed and spread over ten seconds, magic damage to a lesser degree.
2. **Purify:** Purifying Brew clears half of your staggered damage. You use it when a lot has built up.
3. **Absorb:** Celestial Brew wraps you in a shield that absorbs damage.
4. **Attack:** Keg Smash hits your target and nearby enemies and slows them, Breath of Fire also sets the enemies it hits on fire.
5. **Emergencies:** Fortifying Brew and Niuzao help in dangerous moments.

## Key Abilities

- [**Stagger**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: delays part of the damage and spreads it over time.
- [**Purifying Brew**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=119582){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: clears half of your staggered damage.
- [**Celestial Brew**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322507){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a strong brew that absorbs damage.
- [**Keg Smash**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=121253){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: smashes a keg on your target and hits nearby enemies.
- [**Breath of Fire**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115181){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: breathes fire on enemies in front of you.
- [**Invoke Niuzao, the Black Ox**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=132578){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: an effigy of the Black Ox fights at your side and takes over part of your staggered damage.

## Lore: Chen Stormstout and the Black Ox

Brewmasters come from Pandaria, where brewing is almost an art form. Their most famous role model is **Chen Stormstout**, a wandering Pandaren brewmaster who fought alongside Thrall and Rexxar in Warcraft III and returned to the homeland of his ancestors in Mists of Pandaria.

The spec is dedicated to **Niuzao**, the Black Ox, one of the four **August Celestials** of Pandaria. Niuzao stands for steadfastness and defiance, and that is exactly what the Brewmaster embodies: it stays on its feet, no matter how hard the blow.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- a unique damage system with Stagger,
- very mobile for a tank,
- lots of active tools, never boring,
- an unmistakable fantasy with humor.

**Weaknesses**

- many buttons and a steep learning curve,
- Stagger is harder to read than a simple shield,
- purifying mistakes are felt right away.

## Who Is the Brewmaster For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want an active, technical tank,
- love Pandaria, martial arts and kegs,
- enjoy figuring out and optimizing systems.

**Probably not if you …**

- want a forgiving start as a tank, in which case the [Protection Paladin](/en/classes/paladin/protection) fits better,
- prefer pressing few buttons.

## Brewmaster, Mistweaver or Windwalker?

The Monk can play all three roles. [Mistweaver](/en/classes/monk/mistweaver) heals with mists, [Windwalker](/en/classes/monk/windwalker) is the fast kung fu fighter.
