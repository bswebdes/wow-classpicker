## How It Feels

Demonology is the summoner fantasy. You do not fight alone, you command an entire army from hell. A Felguard with a mighty axe stands at your side permanently, Wild Imps leap out of demonic meteors, Dreadstalkers charge in, and at the climax a Tyrant appears and makes them all stronger.

It feels chaotic and satisfying at the same time. The screen fills with demons, but underneath there is a clear rhythm of building, summoning and unloading.

## How Demonology Works

Demonology uses **Soul Shards**:

1. **Build Soul Shards:** Shadow Bolt generates one Soul Shard, Demonbolt even two.
2. **Summon imps:** Hand of Gul'dan spends Soul Shards and drops a meteor full of Wild Imps on the target.
3. **Dreadstalkers:** Call Dreadstalkers summons two demon hounds that attack your target.
4. **Tyrant:** Summon Demonic Tyrant calls your strongest demon. The more minions are active, the more damage it deals.
5. **Groups:** Implosion pulls your imps to the target and makes them explode.

## Key Abilities

- [**Summon Felguard**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=30146){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your permanent Felguard, a powerful melee fighter.
- [**Shadow Bolt**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=686){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your basic spell, generates a Soul Shard.
- [**Demonbolt**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=264178){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a stronger spell that generates two Soul Shards.
- [**Hand of Gul'dan**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=105174){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: a demonic meteor full of Wild Imps.
- [**Call Dreadstalkers**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=104316){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: two demon hounds attack your target.
- [**Summon Demonic Tyrant**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265187){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: your mightiest demon, stronger with every active minion.
- [**Implosion**](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196277){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}: makes your imps explode at the target.

## Lore: Gul'dan and the Skull of the Man'ari

Demonology carries **Gul'dan**'s name in its most important ability. The orc Warlock made a pact with the Burning Legion, founded the **Shadow Council** and turned the orcs into tools of the demons. To this day he is the epitome of the Warlock who puts power above everything.

In Legion, Demonology Warlocks carried the **Skull of the Man'ari**, the skull of Gul'dan himself, as their artifact. With it they commanded demons with the voice of their most infamous master.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths**

- the strongest pet fantasy in the game,
- very comfortable solo thanks to the Felguard,
- a clear rhythm with a big climax,
- lots of demons on screen, very entertaining.

**Weaknesses**

- a lot going on on screen, which can be confusing,
- the timing of the Tyrant matters a lot,
- cloth, vulnerable to direct hits.

## Who Is the Demonology Warlock For?

**A good fit if you …**

- want to command minions and lead an army,
- love chaos and demons,
- like building toward a big climax.

**Probably not if you …**

- prefer a clean screen, in which case [Destruction](/en/classes/warlock/destruction) fits,
- would rather stand in melee with your minions, for example as an [Unholy Death Knight](/en/classes/death-knight/unholy).

## Demonology, Affliction or Destruction?

[Affliction](/en/classes/warlock/affliction) lets enemies wither under curses, [Destruction](/en/classes/warlock/destruction) relies on fire and chaos. Demonology is the choice for everyone who wants to command demons.
