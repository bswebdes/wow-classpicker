## Playstyle

The Shaman places **totems** of the four elements that empower the group or hinder enemies. It heals with **Chain Heal**, throws lightning and empowers its weapon, for example with **Windfury**. **Reincarnation** lets it bring itself back to life.

## Talent trees

- **Elemental** focuses on lightning and fire spells from range.
- **Enhancement** turns the Shaman into a melee fighter with heavy weapon strikes.
- **Restoration** is the healing tree with strong group healing.

## Who is the Shaman for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** want to stay flexible and like supporting your group with totems.

**Not ideal if you …** want to tank.

## New in Forever

The **Dwarf Shaman** is new, giving the Alliance Shamans for the first time. On the Horde side, the **Skyborne** can also be Shamans.
