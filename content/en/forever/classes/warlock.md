## Playstyle

The Warlock applies **curses** and damage over time and summons **demons** such as the Imp, Voidwalker or Succubus. It collects **Soul Shards**, creates **Healthstones** and **Soulstones** and can summon teammates. [**Drain Life**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} keeps it alive.

## Talent trees

- **Affliction** improves curses and damage over time.
- **Demonology** empowers the demons.
- **Destruction** focuses on direct fire damage.

## Who is the Warlock for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** level solo a lot, like companions and enjoy the dark fantasy.

**Not ideal if you …** want to tank or heal.
