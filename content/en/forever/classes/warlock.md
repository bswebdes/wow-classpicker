## Playstyle

The Warlock applies **curses** and damage over time and summons **demons** such as the Imp, Voidwalker or Succubus. It collects **Soul Shards**, creates **Healthstones** and **Soulstones** and can summon teammates. [**Drain Life**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=689){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} keeps it alive.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, the warlock learns every demon through a class quest of its own: first the Voidwalker, later the Succubus and Felhunter. At level 40 a quest grants the Felsteed, and in Classic the epic Dreadsteed came after a long quest chain in Dire Maul. Hardly any class had so many memorable class quests.

## Talent trees

### Affliction

Affliction improves curses and damage over time. You afflict enemies with Corruption and Siphon Life and move on to the next while the first is still withering. Very efficient for leveling.

**Signature abilities:** [**Corruption**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=172){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Siphon Life**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18265){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Demonology

Demonology strengthens your demons. Soul Link shares damage with your companion, and Demonic Sacrifice trades it for a strong bonus. The sturdiest way to play a warlock.

**Signature abilities:** [**Soul Link**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19028){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Demonic Sacrifice**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18788){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Destruction

Destruction is about direct fire and shadow damage. Shadowburn finishes enemies in one blow, and Conflagrate ignites them again. In Classic the choice for raids.

**Signature abilities:** [**Shadowburn**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17877){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Conflagrate**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17962){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
