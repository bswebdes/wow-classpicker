## Playstyle

The Druid shifts into **Bear**, **Cat**, **Travel** and **Aquatic Form**. In caster form it casts nature spells and heals mostly with effects over time. **Rebirth** brings allies back in combat, and **Innervate** helps healers with mana.

## Talent trees

- **Balance** improves nature spells from range.
- **Feral Combat** empowers Bear and Cat Form and covers both tanking and melee damage.
- **Restoration** is the healing tree.

## Who is the Druid for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** don't want to commit to one role and enjoy switching forms.

**Not ideal if you …** want a simple class that is quick to learn.

## New in Forever

Besides Night Elves and Tauren, the **Skyborne** can now be Druids on both sides.
