## Playstyle

::class-art{name="druid"}
::

The Druid shifts into **Bear**, **Cat**, **Travel** and **Aquatic Form**. In caster form it casts nature spells and heals mostly with effects over time. [**Rebirth**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20484){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} brings allies back in combat, and [**Innervate**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=29166){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} helps healers with mana.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, druids earn their forms: class quests teach Bear Form and Aquatic Form, and the journey keeps leading back to **Moonglade**, where druids can teleport at any time. In Classic only night elves and tauren could become druids. As a hybrid, the druid can tank, heal and deal damage, often all in one evening.

## Talent trees

### Balance

Balance improves nature spells from range. With talents you become a Moonkin and cover enemies in an Insect Swarm. A niche in Classic, but with a very distinct fantasy.

**Signature abilities:** [**Moonkin Form**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=24858){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Insect Swarm**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5570){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Feral Combat

Feral Combat strengthens bear and cat and covers both tanking and melee damage. As a cat you shred, as a bear you hold the group together, and Leader of the Pack empowers everyone nearby.

**Signature abilities:** [**Shred**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5221){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Leader of the Pack**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17007){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Restoration

Restoration is the healing tree. Swiftmend consumes a heal over time for instant help, and Innervate gives a healer fresh mana. A very sought-after group healer in Classic.

**Signature abilities:** [**Swiftmend**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18562){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Innervate**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=29166){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
