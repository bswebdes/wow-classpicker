## Playstyle

The Hunter fights from range with a bow or gun and has a **tamed companion** at its side. **Aspects** adjust its style, for more attack power or faster travel, and [**Feign Death**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5384){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and **traps** help when things get tight.

## Talent trees

- **Beast Mastery** empowers the pet and is the classic leveling tree.
- **Marksmanship** improves ranged shots and was the typical raid choice in Classic.
- **Survival** focuses on traps, agility and control.

## Who is the Hunter for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** play a lot solo, want to collect pets or are new to Classic.

**Not ideal if you …** want to tank or heal.

## New in Forever

The **Human Hunter** is a new combination, and the **Skyborne** can be Hunters on both sides.
