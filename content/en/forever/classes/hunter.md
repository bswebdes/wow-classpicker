## Playstyle

::class-art{name="hunter"}
::

The Hunter fights from range with a bow or gun and has a **tamed companion** at its side. **Aspects** adjust its style, for more attack power or faster travel, and [**Feign Death**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=5384){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and **traps** help when things get tight.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, every hunter starts by looking for a first pet: from level 10, a class quest teaches you how to tame. After that you roam the world in search of rare companions, from ghost wolves to big cats. Those who made it all the way could earn the epic bow **Rhok'delar** in Classic through a long quest chain.

## Talent trees

### Beast Mastery

Beast Mastery strengthens your pet and is the classic leveling tree. Your pet holds enemies in place while you shoot from a safe distance. Bestial Wrath makes it unstoppable for a while, and Intimidation stuns the enemy.

**Signature abilities:** [**Bestial Wrath**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19574){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Intimidation**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19577){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Marksmanship

Marksmanship improves ranged shots and was the typical raid choice in Classic. Aimed Shot hits hard, and Trueshot Aura strengthens the whole group.

**Signature abilities:** [**Aimed Shot**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19434){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Trueshot Aura**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19506){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Scatter Shot**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19503){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Survival

Survival is about traps, agility and control. Deterrence lets you dodge attacks, and traps keep enemies at bay. A tree for everyone who likes to play tactically.

**Signature abilities:** [**Deterrence**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=19263){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
