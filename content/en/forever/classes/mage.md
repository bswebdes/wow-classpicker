## Playstyle

::class-art{name="mage"}
::

The Mage casts arcane, fire and frost spells from range. It conjures **food and water**, later opens **portals** to the capital cities and takes enemies out with [**Polymorph**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. [**Frost Nova**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Blink**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} keep enemies at a distance.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, the mage is the scholar among adventurers, trained in Stormwind, the Undercity or by the Kirin Tor. Mages conjure food and water for the whole group, opens portals to the capitals and turns enemies into sheep. In Classic that made them welcome in every group, not just for their damage.

## Talent trees

### Arcane

Arcane improves mana and utility. Presence of Mind makes your next spell instant, and Arcane Power briefly boosts your damage. Usually combined with Frost or Fire.

**Signature abilities:** [**Presence of Mind**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12043){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Arcane Power**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12042){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Fire

Fire is about big hits: Pyroblast as an opener, Blast Wave against groups and Combustion for a stream of critical hits. Lots of damage, but also lots of threat.

**Signature abilities:** [**Pyroblast**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11366){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Blast Wave**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11113){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Combustion**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11129){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Frost

Frost is the tree for leveling and control. Ice Barrier protects you, Ice Block saves you from certain death, and Cold Snap resets your frost spells. Freezing enemies and keeping them at bay, that is Frost.

**Signature abilities:** [**Ice Barrier**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11426){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Ice Block**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=11958){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Cold Snap**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=12472){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
