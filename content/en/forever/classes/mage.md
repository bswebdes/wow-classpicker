## Playstyle

The Mage casts arcane, fire and frost spells from range. It conjures **food and water**, later opens **portals** to the capital cities and takes enemies out with [**Polymorph**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=118){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. [**Frost Nova**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=122){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Blink**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1953){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} keep enemies at a distance.

## Talent trees

- **Arcane** improves mana and arcane spells.
- **Fire** focuses on big hits and critical strikes.
- **Frost** brings control and slows and is great for leveling.

## Who is the Mage for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** want to cast spells from range and control groups of enemies.

**Not ideal if you …** want to tank or heal or need a lot of sturdiness.

## New in Forever

The **Orc Mage** is a new combination, and Alliance **Skyborne** can be Mages too.

Test the Mage in the [Forever arena](/en/forever) or roll a combination with the [random generator](/en/forever/random-class-generator).
