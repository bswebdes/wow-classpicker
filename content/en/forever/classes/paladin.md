## Playstyle

::class-art{name="paladin"}
::

The Paladin fights with **Seals** and **Judgements**, empowers allies with **Blessings** and gives the group **Auras**. Thanks to [**Divine Shield**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=642){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Lay on Hands**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=633){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} it is extremely hard to kill, even if its leveling damage is on the slow side.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, paladins are the knights of the Alliance, trained by the **Order of the Silver Hand** and hardened in the fight against the Scourge in the Plaguelands. Typical of Classic: paladins earned their warhorse through a class quest, and the epic charger quest led all the way to Scholomance. Paladins bless their group, cleanse poisons and diseases and can resurrect the fallen.

## Talent trees

### Holy

Holy is the most popular specialization: an efficient healer who is welcome in every group. Holy Shock gives you an instant heal, Divine Favor a guaranteed critical hit and Consecration damage all around you.

**Signature abilities:** [**Holy Shock**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20473){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Divine Favor**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20216){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Consecration**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=26573){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Protection

Protection is the tank tree. In Classic it was a niche, and WoW Forever reworks the talent trees with new milestone talents. Holy Shield and Blessing of Kings, which the whole group loves, are typical.

**Signature abilities:** [**Holy Shield**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20925){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Blessing of Kings**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20217){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Retribution

Retribution deals melee damage with a two-handed weapon. Seal of Command adds heavy extra hits, and Repentance takes an enemy out of the fight for a moment. Relaxed for leveling, with lots of armor and self-healing.

**Signature abilities:** [**Seal of Command**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20375){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Repentance**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20066){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

## Who is the Paladin for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** want to heal, like supporting groups and want a class that rarely dies.

**Not ideal if you …** want to progress quickly solo or deal lots of damage.

## New in Forever

The **Undead Paladin** is one of the new race and class combinations. That makes the Paladin playable on both sides for the first time, alongside Humans and Dwarves for the Alliance.
