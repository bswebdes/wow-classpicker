## Playstyle

::class-art{name="rogue"}
::

The Rogue sneaks up in [**Stealth**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1784){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, opens with a surprise attack and uses **Energy** and **Combo Points** to build toward strong finishers. [**Sap**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=6770){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Blind**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=2094){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Vanish**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1856){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} let it control the fight, and it also has **poisons**, pickpocketing and lockpicking.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, rogues learn their craft in secret: poisons only come after a class quest of their own, and lockpicking opens chests that stay closed to everyone else. The rogue guild **Ravenholdt** in the Alterac Mountains is neutral and takes in thieves from both factions. Rogues sneak, lurk and strike from stealth.

## Talent trees

### Assassination

Assassination improves poisons and critical hits. Cold Blood guarantees that your next finisher crits. Ideal for everyone who likes to plan one big hit.

**Signature abilities:** [**Cold Blood**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14177){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Combat

Combat is the classic tree for steady damage with swords or daggers, popular for leveling and raiding. Blade Flurry hits two enemies at once, and Adrenaline Rush gives you energy to spare.

**Signature abilities:** [**Blade Flurry**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13877){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Adrenaline Rush**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=13750){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Subtlety

Subtlety improves stealth and is strong in PvP. Premeditation lets you open with extra combo points, Preparation resets your cooldowns, and Hemorrhage strengthens your group's hits.

**Signature abilities:** [**Premeditation**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14183){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Preparation**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14185){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Hemorrhage**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=16511){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
