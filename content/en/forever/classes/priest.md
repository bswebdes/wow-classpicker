## Playstyle

::class-art{name="priest"}
::

The Priest heals with big spells, protects with [**Power Word: Shield**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=17){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and buffs the group with [**Power Word: Fortitude**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=1243){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. In [**Shadowform**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15473){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} it becomes a damage dealer with damage over time. [**Mind Control**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=605){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [**Psychic Scream**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=8122){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} provide control.

## Background and lore

In the classic world, every priest race had spells of its own: dwarves protected against fear with **Fear Ward**, night elves called down Starshards, undead spread a devouring plague. So the same priest felt a little different for every race. Those who played to the end could earn an epic staff of Light or Shadow through a quest chain from Molten Core.

## Talent trees

### Discipline

Discipline strengthens shields, mana and support. Power Infusion briefly makes an ally stronger, and Inner Focus makes your next spell free. A tree for players who like to make others shine.

**Signature abilities:** [**Power Infusion**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=10060){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Inner Focus**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=14751){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Holy

Holy is the pure healer. Lightwell lets you place a source of healing, and even after death Spirit of Redemption keeps healing. The classic raid healer.

**Signature abilities:** [**Lightwell**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=724){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Spirit of Redemption**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=20711){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

### Shadow

Shadow is the damage tree. In Shadowform you flay minds, inflict pain and heal yourself through Vampiric Embrace. The most comfortable choice for leveling a priest.

**Signature abilities:** [**Shadowform**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15473){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Mind Flay**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15407){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [**Vampiric Embrace**](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=15286){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
