## Playstyle

The Priest heals with big spells, protects with **Power Word: Shield** and buffs the group with **Power Word: Fortitude**. In **Shadowform** it becomes a damage dealer with damage over time. **Mind Control** and **Psychic Scream** provide control.

## Talent trees

- **Discipline** improves shields, mana efficiency and support.
- **Holy** is the pure healer's tree.
- **Shadow** unlocks Shadowform and is the best choice for leveling.

## Who is the Priest for in Forever?

**A good fit if you …** want to heal and play a class groups always need.

**Not ideal if you …** want a sturdy solo class, since the Priest is vulnerable in cloth.
